🛠️ About the Developers: From Workbench to Code ​

OmniCraft ERP is not the product of a faceless tech corporation or another venture-funded cloud subscription. This program was born amidst the dust and scent of genuine leather, under the rhythmic strike of a mallet and the hum of sewing machines.

The system is developed by the N. Murashkin Leather Workshop.

🌐 Workshop Website: nmurashkin.ru

🧵 Philosophy of Craft and Code ​

In our workshop, we follow a strict rule: to create items that will bring joy to their owners for decades. We do not chase fast fashion or disposable goods. Each bag, wallet, or passport cover is engineered for long-term service, utilizing top-grade leather and robust hardware.

We have carried this same philosophy of "enduring value" into our software development. OmniCraft ERP was created as a tool for life for the artisan. We believe that business accounting software should be:

  • Reliable: Like a strong stitch with waxed thread, the software must not tear when you need it most. Our database architecture (WAL-journaling, GFS-backups) ensures the safety of your metrics.
  • Independent: Your data belongs entirely to you. That is why OmniCraft ERP works locally on your computer (Local-First), requires no constant internet connection, and never transmits your financial secrets to external servers.
  • Honest: We do not force you to maintain bookkeeping records just for the sake of it. The program speaks to you in the language of business logic, cost prices, and your return on time (Time ROI).

💡 Why We Created OmniCraft ERP ​

Before this program was created, our workshop records were scattered across cumbersome Excel spreadsheets, notebooks, and disconnected applications. Every time we needed to calculate the exact cost of a backpack—taking into account leather shipping, tool wear, and hours spent—it required tens of minutes of redundant math.

We realized that an artisan needs something different:

  1. Friction-Free Automation: The program itself should calculate the MAC-based cost price of materials, allocate shipping, and calculate depreciation.
  2. Understanding Efficiency: The master needs to see more than just "income and expenses"; they need to understand Time ROI (return on time) and their monthly break-even point.
  3. Freedom from the Cloud: We wanted to be sure that our service would never shut down overnight, raise prices tenfold, or lock us out of our own client database.

Thus, OmniCraft ERP was born—a smart ERP built by crafters, for crafters (and any other business-minded artisans).
