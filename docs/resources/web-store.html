Online Storefront & Web Showcase ​

The built-in "Showcase" module transforms your product catalog, service list, and warehouse inventory into an autonomous, lightning-fast, and modern online storefront. You get an independent public platform free from recurring platform fees and vulnerable server-side content management systems.

The storefront is compiled using modern Jamstack static site generation (SSG). The output is a clean bundle of autonomous files: HTML5 markup, CSS3 styling, vanilla JavaScript logic, and modern WebP and AVIF raster graphics.

The resulting website opens instantly on mobile devices and desktop computers, requires no server database, and can be hosted for free or minimal cost on high-availability infrastructures: S3 object storage buckets, direct Cloudflare Pages deployment, GitHub Pages, or traditional hosting environments via FTP.

Architecture of Independence: Advantages of a Sovereign Showcase ​

The storefront is engineered specifically for craftspeople, makers, service engineers, and local studios. The architecture removes intermediaries between creator and buyer.

Freedom from Third-Party Subscriptions and Commissions

Unlike cloud website builders, an OmniCraft ERP storefront cannot be disabled by platform bans, does not extract transaction commissions, and never injects third-party advertisements. Your customer database, source assets, and deployed site remain 100% under your ownership.

Showcase Structure and Capabilities ​

1. Universal Modular Home Page ​

The home page adapts seamlessly across diverse crafts — from bespoke leather goods and wood ateliers to electronic repair centers and hybrid studios. Every section is independently configurable:

  • Top Notification Ribbon: Optional header banner for holiday announcements, lead times, or booking slots. Visitors can dismiss the bar, and their preference persists throughout their session.
  • Three Hero Layouts:
    • "Triptych" — balanced three-column composition highlighting signature works;
    • "Split Screen" — modern 50/50 balance between brand narrative and imagery;
    • "Wide Banner" — cinematic full-width hero with balanced contrast darkening.
  • Manual Banner Slider: Supports 1 to 3 curated slides with manual control (navigation arrows, indicator dots, mobile touch swipes). Intrusive automatic scrolling is disabled so visitors can examine details without distraction. If custom banners are absent, the system displays your first published item automatically.
  • Banner Inquiry Action: When Telegram or WhatsApp accounts are enabled, the wide banner presents an instant "Ask a Question" button that connects the client directly to your messenger.
  • Twelve Modular Sections: Trust badge row with vector iconography, category gallery with 3:4 portrait cards, new arrivals and featured items, compact tabbed price list, full-width promo banner, production stages, the "Craftsman Note" section (with portrait, credentials, and facsimile signature), journal stories, customer testimonials, interactive FAQ accordions, and a concluding messenger invitation.
  • Full-Screen Navigation Overlay: The menu toggle opens a frosted glass full-screen overlay. Structure is divided into two distinct columns: catalog departments on the left, brand story, shipping policies, and direct contact channels on the right.
  • Adaptive Navigation Floating Button: Hidden on the initial screen to avoid visual clutter. As the user scrolls, a subtle floating button appears pointing toward footer contact channels, flipping upwards in lower sections for an immediate return to the top.

2. Item & Service Detail Page ​

Each item page reflects deep appreciation for high craftsmanship and engineering precision:

  • Kinematic 3D Triptych Gallery: Full-width first-screen slider with simulated depth perspective. The active central frame is flanked by angled previews, offering a tactile impression of scale and materials.
  • Floating Discovery Badge: Positioned over the lower border of the gallery, presenting the item name, price tag, an anchor link to specifications, and an interactive modifier trigger ("OTHER COLORS", "LEATHER OPTIONS").
  • Two-Column Detail Layout:
    • Sticky Purchase Column: Stays pinned during scrolling. Keeps prominent pricing, customizable prefixes ("from "), option selectors, a substantial cart button, instant messenger shortcuts, and shipping conditions in clear view.
    • Craft Narrative Column: Storytelling area featuring classic drop caps, bulleted technical highlights, direct maker quotes, and modular content blocks.
  • Modular Storytelling Blocks:
    • Introductory Lead: Concise, evocative overview set in large typography;
    • Feature Grid: Icon cards detailing material selection and construction techniques;
    • Making-of Story: Narrative detailing leather carving, metal tempering, or bespoke calibration;
    • Secondary Lookbook: High-resolution gallery capturing the item in everyday context;
    • Lightweight Video Player: Privacy-respecting facade for VK, Rutube, or YouTube videos (external scripts do not load until the user clicks play);
    • Technical Specifications: Structured data table covering dimensions, raw materials, hardware grades, and net weight;
    • Product FAQs: Expandable accordion answering maintenance and warranty inquiries.

3. Sovereign Checkout & End-to-End Data Protection ​

OmniCraft ERP implements a privacy-first checkout pipeline that keeps buyer data shielded from third-party tracking:

  1. Three Customer Contact Options:
    • Direct Telegram Chat: Launches your messenger with a pre-filled item order summary;
    • Direct WhatsApp Chat: Instantly opens a chat containing selected options and total cost;
    • Serverless Web Cart: Familiar browser shopping cart operating entirely client-side.
  2. Client-Side End-to-End Encryption (E2EE):
    • Customer details (full name, phone number, shipping address, notes) are encrypted inside the customer's browser using AES-256-GCM.
    • The symmetric encryption key is sealed using an asymmetric RSA-OAEP 2048-bit algorithm with the storefront's public key generated during compilation.
    • The private decryption key is stored strictly within your local OmniCraft ERP database on your personal computer, secured by the Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI).
    • Neither the web host nor network intermediaries possess the capability to decrypt customer personal data.
  3. Dual Gateway Transport:
    • Official Telegram Bot API: Secure transmission of encrypted payloads directly into your studio channel;
    • Server-side checkout: Submits the order through a checkout endpoint on a host that supports PHP.
  4. Legal Compliance Suite:
    • The compiler generates standard Privacy Policy (/pages/privacy) and Terms of Service (/pages/terms) templates populated with your registered business credentials.
    • The web checkout includes a mandatory consent checkbox linked directly to both legal agreements.

Browser checkout makes the bot token public

When you select the Telegram Bot channel, the storefront publishes the bot token and chat ID in its page source even when orders are encrypted. Server-side checkout and direct messenger chat publish pages without these credentials. If the token was already visible on published pages, rotate it, switch to another order channel, and republish the storefront.

The storefront confirms the order once the bot accepts the message. If delivery cannot be confirmed, the cart and completed fields stay on the page. Add a public Telegram or WhatsApp contact so customers can reach you directly if checkout fails.

Choose your uploaded logo palette (light, dark, or multicolored) for contrast across themes. Set the default theme and choose a product backdrop or leave it transparent. Provide an international phone number, business address, and links to your social channels.

    • Directions grid. In the editor for each category you want to include, select “Show in directions grid”. On the “Home Page” tab, open “Categories & Directions Grid”, turn on the block, enter the title and subtitle, and arrange the cards. Click “Save Changes”. If the selection changed while you were reordering the cards, reopen site settings and arrange them again.
    • Best sellers. On the Home Page tab, expand the Featured / Best Sellers Block. Automatic ranks items by units sold across all channels. Sales period defaults to 90 days. Choose another preset or set dates under Custom range.

The marks are saved to the product cards immediately. If the marked products fill the block, they take every slot. Select “Marked only” to leave the remaining slots empty. Both modes include only products customers can see.

In auto and compact modes, “Items Limit” sets a cap of 2 to 48 items. Full mode shows every item visible to customers. Auto mode hides the catalog when more than 12 items are visible and the home page already shows best sellers or new arrivals.
    • Card labels. Set the default price prefix and button text in the same block. Labels set on an individual product card take precedence. Click “Save Changes” and check the result in the preview. If an external agent changed the catalog settings, reopen the editor and apply your edits again.
    • Craftsman note. On the “Home Page” tab, expand “Craftsman Word / Manifesto”. Turn on the block, then enter a title, author caption, and quote. Click “Upload Portrait” to choose a photo. Use “Add Metric” to include a fact such as 15 years of experience. Click “Save Changes” and review the section in the preview. If an external agent edited the same block after you opened the form, reopen the editor and apply your edits again.
    • Journal on the home page. Open “Journal & Articles on Home”, turn on the block, enter a title, and set “Items Limit” to 1–9 posts. Click “Save Changes” and check the preview. The block appears when the site journal is enabled and the selected storefront has published posts. The link at the top of the block opens the full journal.
    • Reviews and examples of work. On the “Home Page” tab, expand “Client Reviews & Cases”. Turn on the block and enter a title and subtitle. Click “Add Review / Case”, fill in “Client Name” and “Review / Case Summary”, and choose a rating from 1 to 5. Add the name of the work and a photo if needed. Arrange the cards, click “Save Changes”, and check the order in the preview. The block is hidden when the list is empty.
    • Frequently asked questions. On the “Home Page” tab, expand “FAQ Accordion”, turn on the block, and click “Add Question”. Enter a question and answer, arrange the cards, then click “Save Changes”. The block appears on the storefront when it contains at least one complete question and answer.

Click “Save Changes” to apply the template and your edits together. If someone has already changed the home page settings, reopen the editor and repeat your edits.
    • Pin best sellers. Click Add manually, search by product name, and select an item. Pinned items appear first. Use Move up and Move down to reorder them. You can also set or clear the same mark under Featured on home in the product card.

If there were no sales, add items manually. Manual mode shows pinned items up to the limit. The site hides the block when the list is empty.

Step-by-Step Creation and Placement ​

  1. Define Block Type & Item: in "Foundation and Design", open "Regions and blocks → Block types". Click "Add block" inside an existing type (or create a custom type via "Add block type" with a chosen display format) and define an internal editor name and public website title.
  2. Populate Content: enter questions and answers for FAQs; provide titles, descriptions, and images for card grids; or specify item thresholds for catalog blocks.
  3. Assign to Region: switch to the "Regions" tab, click "Add block" next to the target region ("End of Content" or "Before the Footer"), and select your configured block.
  4. Targeting & Granular Filtering: select matching page categories ("Product cards", "Publications", "Standard pages"). Optionally add explicit pages via "Add individual page" or define suppressions via "Exclude individual page" (exclusion rules always override inclusions).
  5. Reorder & Publish: reorder items with "Move up" / "Move down", enable via "Show block", save changes, and verify the presentation in Live Preview.

TIP

Content and block parameters update across all active placements simultaneously upon the next site build. Clicking "Remove from region" simply detaches the block from that region while keeping it in the library for future reuse.

Product Attributes and Characteristic Groups ​

Storefront shoppers filter craft items by specific attributes: color, size, leather type, hardware finish, and compatibility. To eliminate manual re-entry of repetitive fields across multiple products, OmniCraft ERP provides a multi-level taxonomy: standalone attributes, reusable attribute groups, and faceted catalog filters.

Purpose and Attribute Group Hierarchy ​

In craft workshops and repair studios, distinct product types frequently share identical technical parameters. For instance, leather belts, bags, and cardholders all require fields like "Leather Grade", "Thread Color", and "Hardware Alloy":

  1. Single Source of Truth: Creating an attribute group once allows linking it across dozens of product types.
  2. Conflict-Free Inheritance: If an attribute exists in both the base product type and an attached group, the system merges them cleanly — buyers see the field on the product card exactly once.
  3. Automated Faceted Filtering: Selected attributes automatically generate sidebar filters in your storefront catalog, allowing visitors to narrow down craft items effortlessly.

Prerequisites ​

Navigate to "Store Management" → "2. CATALOG & CONTENT" → "Catalog Settings" sub-tab.

Step-by-Step Guide to Attribute Groups and Catalog Filtering ​

  1. Define Standalone Attributes:
    • Open "Facets" → “All Attributes”. Search by attribute name or code;
    • Click "Add Attribute";
    • Specify system key, public storefront label, and field type (text string, numeric with units, or selectable option list);
    • Check "In Catalog Filters" for parameters you want shoppers to filter by;
    • Click "Save".
  2. Create an Attribute Group:
    • In "Facets", switch to “Attribute Groups”;
    • Select “Create Group” (the plus button);
    • In the modal dialog, enter a descriptive title in "Group name" (e.g., "Leather & Hardware Specs", "Physical Dimensions");
    • Select the checkboxes for the relevant attributes from the catalog list;
    • Click "Save". The group appears in the registry displaying its assigned fields.
  3. Attach Groups to Product Types:
    • Open the "Product Types" sub-tab;
    • Click the pencil edit icon next to your desired product type (e.g., "Handmade Belts");
    • Under the groups section, attach your created attribute group and click "Save";
    • All group attributes become immediately available for data entry across all product cards assigned to that type.
  4. Order Storefront Catalog Filters:
    • Switch to the "Categories" sub-tab;
    • In the target category editor, locate "Filter order by codes";
    • Enter comma-separated attribute keys in your preferred display order (e.g., color, leather_type, buckle_width);
    • Leaving the field empty instructs the static generator to automatically sort filters based on active category stock.
  5. Edit and Delete Attribute Groups:
    • To update attributes, click "Edit Group" on the group row. Adjustments sync automatically across all attached product types;
    • To remove a group, click "Delete". If the group is attached to live products with populated attributes, safety validations prevent accidental data loss and report active dependencies.

Global Core Attributes

Add shared attributes such as country of origin to the base product type. Add fields specific to one type, such as belt length, in that type's editor.

Step 2. Preparing Catalog Items for Publication ​

  1. Open “Storefront Products”. A progress bar at the top of the section stays visible while the product list loads.
  2. Toggle between the visual 3:4 portrait card grid and the compact tabular ledger.
  3. Click any card to enter the multi-tab item editor:
    • 1. General & Pricing: Set the retail price, optional “from” prefix, badge, page address, category, and model.
    • 2. Media (photos): Add product photography, reorder angles, and assign the cover shot ("★ Cover").
    • 3. Specifications & Delivery: Fill in catalog filter attributes, set shipping packaging, parcel dimensions, and gross weight.
    • 4. Order Options: Configure selectable buyer variations (dimensions, personalized engraving, gift box packaging).
    • 5. Landing & Sections: Draft rich Markdown narrative and attach promotional showcase blocks (features, craft story, video demo, FAQ).
  4. Click “Save”. When the card is ready, turn on “Publish on website” in the catalog list or inside the card.

If you have yet to save the card, clicking the switch in the list opens the editor. Save a card separately for each storefront where you want to publish the product.

Control stock availability and ordering ​

Open “Site Management” → “Categories & Items” → “Product Types”. Open the base “Product” type and set the shared rules under “Storefront availability and visibility”. Free stock excludes reserved units.

  1. Under “When the item is in stock”, choose whether to show the label and set its text. A separate checkbox shows the exact number of free units on the product page.
  2. Under “When out of stock”, choose one of the three options.

Set separate rules for a type or product ​

  1. On a child type, clear “Use base type settings” if the group needs its own rules.
  2. For a single product, open “General & Pricing” and clear “Use product type settings”. Change the settings you need. To restore the shared settings, select the checkbox again and save the card.
  3. Update the published storefront. At checkout it checks the saved cart and asks shoppers to remove unavailable items or reduce the quantity.

Set addresses for products of the same type ​

  1. Open “Site Management” → “Categories & Items” → “Product Types” and edit a product type.
  2. Set the shared template on the base “Product” type. The default address uses the product name. For a child type, leave “Use the base type’s address template” checked or clear it to build a custom template. The preview uses the current storefront’s domain and the name of the type you are editing.
  3. A new card fills in “Product page address” using the effective template. Empty values and attributes disabled for the type are skipped. If all parts are empty, the address comes from the product name. You can also enter the address manually.

When saving a template with matching product pages on the current storefront, choose “Only for new product pages” or “Review new addresses…”. For the base type, this includes its own product pages and those of child types that inherit its template. For a single product page, click the generate button next to the address field and save the page.

Update links for several products ​

  1. Under “Product Types”, select “Update product links”. Choose all types, a specific type, or “Base type and inheriting types”.
  2. Select “Only products with automatic links (recommended)” to keep addresses entered manually in this version. “Include all products” also includes those pages.
  3. Select “Show changes”. Compare the old and new addresses, clear the checkboxes for any products you want to skip, and click “Apply changes”.
  4. Rebuild and publish the current storefront to keep previously published links pointing to the new addresses. The build includes _redirects rules for hosts that support them and individual redirect pages.

When old links stop working

When an address changes, the product history keeps up to five of its most recent redirects. Before a build, the storefront retires entries older than 365 days and entries for deleted or archived products. A build includes up to 1,950 of the newest rules for published products. This leaves room under the Cloudflare Pages limit of 2,000 static rules.

Change the template when you need to restructure your catalog addresses. Frequent trials can displace links that customers still use to reach the storefront.

Addresses saved in earlier versions

After upgrading, existing addresses are treated as automatic. Before the first bulk update, review the list and clear the checkboxes for links you want to keep. Entering an address manually and saving the page marks it as custom. The generate button switches it back to automatic.

Link cards that share a product model ​

  1. Select the same “Product model” in each product recipe. For a stock item without a recipe, use this field in “Receive Finished Goods”. The pencil button opens the model manager where you can create, rename, or delete models.
  2. In the storefront card’s “Product model” field, keep “From inventory” to inherit the model or choose another model for that storefront.
  3. In “Linked Items”, enable variants and select “By Product Model”. Published cards with the same product type and model show a button for switching between variants.

After you build the site, the selected model appears in the product specifications. If you previously entered the model only as text in the specifications, select it from the model list in the product card and rebuild the site.

Where to find product models

Open “Site Management” → “Categories & Items” → “Product models”. The list shows how many recipes and stock items are linked to each model.

Link products by code or attribute ​

  • By Variant Group Code. Enter the same “Variant group code” on the relevant product cards. Select “By Variant Group Code”. This can link products across models and product types.
  • By Attribute. Select a field such as “By Attribute: Collection” and give it the same value on the relevant product cards.

The button appears when a group contains at least two published products. “Show badges” displays the product class and promotional badge. To show color, material, or size, turn on “Show Attributes” and select the relevant fields.

If you previously grouped by model or code

On the next build, “By Product Model” will group cards that share a model and product type. To keep manually defined groups, select “By Variant Group Code”, save the settings, and rebuild the storefront.

Step 3. Local Live Preview ​

Before publishing to the internet, audit your storefront in a local browser:

  1. In the top action bar, click "Live Preview" (eye icon).
  2. OmniCraft ERP compiles your active database into static assets, starts a lightweight local web server, and opens your browser.
  3. Test checkout flows, verify responsiveness across window sizes, and switch themes.
  4. Open a category and select several of its subcategories. Check for a breadcrumb such as Store / Products / Belts: Unstitched, Stitched. The Belts link clears the subcategory selection. The Products link opens the full catalog and clears the filters.

Protection Against Stale Browser Cache

The built-in preview server sends strict no-cache headers. Any subsequent updates saved inside OmniCraft ERP become visible immediately upon refreshing the page (F5).

Step 4. Site Deployment & Transport Selection ​

Under "Site Administration", open "Site Deployment" and choose your deployment method:

  • Direct Cloudflare Pages Upload: The fastest modern transport. Bypasses Git commits by uploading only modified static files through SHA-256 checksum comparison. Includes automated SSL certification and global CDN performance.
  • S3 Object Storage: Scalable, reliable static hosting with support for custom domains and automated synchronization.
  • Custom Web Hosting. Enter the server address, port, username, password, and the site's directory on the server. If your hosting provider supports encrypted file transfer over FTPS, enable it in the deployment settings.
  • Local Folder or ZIP Archive: Exports a complete static distribution to your drive for offline distribution or manual backup.
  • GitHub Pages: Publishes directly to the gh-pages branch without manual terminal commands.

Click “Test Connection”, then “Save Transport Settings”, and click “Publish Website” in the header. After publishing, click “Open Website”. If you use a custom domain, connect it with your hosting provider first.

Unified Order Inbox: Processing Customer Deals ​

All inbound inquiries across your storefront and external channels converge in the "Order Inbox" sub-tab:

  1. Telegram Bot Synchronization: Clicking "🔄 Sync" queries the Telegram Bot API, downloads new payloads, decrypts them with your local RSA key, standardizes phone numbers to E.164, eliminates duplicates, and automatically registers clients into your CRM database.
  2. Smart Clipboard Parsing: When conversing on Avito, social networks, or messengers, simply copy the client's message to your clipboard and click "Smart Paste". The system extracts the customer name, phone number, delivery address, and requested items.
  3. Quick Reply Templates: The "Reply" button opens pre-formatted responses ("Order Confirmed", "Package Dispatched", "Payment Details") and launches the client's chat.
  4. One-Click Stock Deduction: Once an order is packed, click "Execute Sale". The application deducts finished inventory batches using the first-in, first-out method (FIFO) and records cash inflow in Cash Flow (DDS).
  5. Two-Phase Inventory Reservation: Incoming orders reserve available inventory immediately, preventing accidental double-selling. If stock is depleted, the order is flagged as "Made to Order". Canceled deals instantly restore reserved items to available inventory.

Troubleshooting & Common Pitfalls ​

What should I do if the storefront fails to render on GitHub Pages after building?

Review the build log under the "Deploy" tab and confirm that GitHub Pages is set to build from "GitHub Actions" under repository Settings → Pages. Verify that your Personal Access Token (PAT) remains valid.

Why does a new product from the Calculator not appear in the web catalog?

In the recipe card, switch to the "Showcase" tab and toggle the "Publish on Web Showcase" switch to active. Save the recipe and trigger a rebuild under the Deploy sub-tab.

How do I resolve Telegram bot order synchronization errors?

Verify that your bot token is accurate in integration settings and confirm that the local private RSA key matches the bot's public key. If keys were rotated, regenerate the keypair in storefront settings.
