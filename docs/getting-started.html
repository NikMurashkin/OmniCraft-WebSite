Quick Start ​

OmniCraft ERP runs locally on your computer with no cloud subscriptions, external servers, or mandatory registration. This guide lays out the direct first-launch sequence: from installing the application and loading initial stock or opening balances to calculating your first manufacturing batch or logging a client service order.

Initial setup takes 10–15 minutes.

Full-featured starter edition with no time expiration. Installs into your user profile without administrator privileges and runs completely offline.

First Launch Roadmap ​

Follow these five steps in sequence: install the application, choose your profile and financial defaults, populate initial stock or migrate existing balances, and only then run your first batch calculation or service order.

  1. Step 1. Installing the Application: user-level deployment without elevated system privileges.
  2. Step 2. Selecting an Accounting Workspace: enable a workspace tailored to production or services and accept the license agreement.
  3. Step 3. Essential Financial Standards: hourly labor rate, global markup, and overhead rules.
  4. Step 4. Initial Inventory and Opening Balances: record equipment, raw materials with purchase cost (starting fresh or from prior records), finished goods, clients, and opening cash.
  5. Step 5. First Practical Operation and Verification: build a recipe from entered materials and calculate a batch, or log and settle a service order.

Step 1. Installing the Application ​

  • System Requirements & User-Level Folder: requires 64-bit Windows. The installer deploys into %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\OmniCraft ERP without prompting for Windows administrator credentials, preventing access conflicts and keeping updates smooth.
  • Quick Setup: run OmniCraft_ERP.exe and click "Install". Files extract automatically, shortcuts are created on your Desktop and Start menu, and you can launch the program immediately.
  • Custom Options: to choose a different folder or skip the Desktop shortcut, click "Installation settings" at the bottom of the installer window.

First launch after an update

If shared materials and equipment stopped appearing in search, the first launch after the update restores search. You can keep using your existing records.

Step 2. Selecting an Accounting Workspace ​

On first launch, the "Welcome to OmniCraft ERP" screen lets you enable an isolated workspace so stock, orders, and cash balances from different business lines never mix:

  • "Production and Craft" (button "Enable Production"): for workshops, makers, and small-batch studios (leather, woodworking, textiles, ceramics, jewelry, etc.). Includes raw materials inventory with moving average cost (MAC), recipe costing calculator, workbench queue, and finished goods stock tracked by FIFO batches.
  • "Services and Maintenance" (button "Enable Services"): for repair shops, studios, detailing centers, and independent specialists. Includes a service price list, service order journal with advance payments, client directory, assets, and consumable stock tracking.

Clicking a profile card opens the "Workspace Activation" confirmation dialog—adjust the workspace name if needed and click "Activate and Start". On your very first launch, the "License Agreement" dialog opens next: scroll down to the end of the text to unlock "I accept the terms". Once accepted, the workspace opens on the "Workshop" tab (or "Assets" in Services).

Workspace Policy by Edition

Free and Standard editions support 1 active workspace at a time; you can archive the current workspace to open a new one (with a 60-minute undo window for accidental archiving). The Professional edition (PRO) unlocks simultaneous multi-workspace operation and permanent archive access. See Licenses & Limitations and General Workspace Information for details.

Step 3. Essential Financial Standards ​

Before adding recipes or pricing orders, switch to the "Settings" tab in the top navigation bar. The system starts with a default hourly rate of 0 and 100% markup—tailor these defaults to match your shop's economics.

Set accounting currency before entering data

Your default currency symbol is automatically detected from your operating system regional settings (e.g., ₽, $, or €). If you plan to use a different accounting currency, choose it via "File ➔ Program Settings" (under "General") before recording initial items in Step 4. Once workspace records (materials, assets, cash transactions) exist, the currency selector is locked to maintain the mathematical accuracy of unit costs and accumulated financial reports.

1. System hourly rate ($/hour) ​

Baseline cost of one labor hour. If you pay assistants or shop staff, enter their average hourly wage so direct labor enters production cost. If you work solo, you can keep this set to 0 and take your earnings from net profit via retail markup.

2. Global Markup (%) ​

Your standard trade markup percentage applied to unit cost when deriving the recommended retail price (RRP) for finished goods.

3. Global Overhead Expense Norms ​

Shop rent, utilities, communication, and other indirect operating costs that must be recovered per unit produced. Click "Add Expense Category" and pick a "Norm Type":

  • "Percentage (%)": calculated as a share of direct raw materials and labor cost.
  • "Fixed ($/unit)": a flat monetary amount added to each finished unit.

Be sure to click "Save Settings" to commit your parameters.

Per-Recipe Multipliers

Global defaults can be scaled up or down inside any product recipe: set a multiplier such as 2.0 for power-heavy equipment or 0 for simple hand assembly.

Step 4. Initial Inventory and Opening Balances ​

You cannot calculate a batch in "Calculator" until raw materials with unit costs exist in your warehouse—the recipe builder requires raw materials from stock. Follow whichever path matches how you are starting today:

  • If starting from scratch: record the tools you already own (without generating a fake cash outflow today), create your material cards, and log your initial material purchase to set baseline prices.
  • If migrating from paper notebooks or spreadsheets ("Cut-off Point"): do not enter years of past receipts and sales. Pick your start date in the app and record an accurate snapshot across the five steps below—your current machinery, raw stock on hand, finished items, clients, and actual cash balances.

1. Equipment, Gear, and Tools ​

Open the "Workshop" tab (in Services — "Assets") and click "Add asset" (or + on the top toolbar). The asset registry works universally across every business line—register machinery, diagnostic devices, studio gear, furniture, tools, and supplies:

  • Card fields: mandatory fields are marked with a red asterisk—enter "Asset Name", "Cost", and for equipment specify "Lifespan" in years (for consumables and shop supplies, simply tick "Consumable"). The "Category" field helps group assets conveniently (or leave it as Uncategorized if preferred).
  • Pre-owned gear vs. new purchase: when entering equipment, devices, or tools bought before adopting OmniCraft ERP, tick "Without tracking in Cash Flow" and optionally set an estimated "Cost" for asset valuation and depreciation—no cash outflow will be booked today. When logging a new purchase (or an initial cash investment), leave that checkbox cleared and fill in "Cost" plus optional "Shipping ($)".

Click "Add Asset".

2. Raw Materials, Inventory, and Parts ​

Open "Raw Materials" (in Services — "Materials"). A material profile stores item metadata only (with zero initial quantity or price), so onboarding inventory requires two quick steps:

  1. Populate directories and create the material profile: click "Add material" (or "+" ("New Raw Material")). Because a fresh workspace starts with empty unit and category lists, click the edit icons next to "Category" ("Manage Categories") and "Unit" ("Manage Units") to add yours first (for example, Leather, Wood, Hardware and pcs, m, kg). Mandatory fields are marked with a red asterisk—enter "Material Name" and select "Unit". The "Category" field helps organize stock and filter items in the recipe builder (you can also leave items as "No category"). Then click "Add".
  2. Record on-hand quantity and unit cost: click the cart icon "Batch Purchase" in the top toolbar (or on the material row). Click "Add Position", pick the material, enter actual "Quantity" and "Total Price" (the total value for that line—plus optional batch "Shipping"), then click "Process". OmniCraft ERP computes the moving average cost (MAC) per unit used by "Calculator".

Why initial priced stock uses Batch Purchase instead of Inventory Act

"Inventory Act" adjusts physical count only and leaves unit cost untouched—if used on an empty profile, unit price stays 0 and "Calculator" shows zero material cost. "Batch Purchase of Raw Materials" captures both stock quantity and valuation (MAC); any cash expense posted to "Cash Flow" for pre-paid historical stock is balanced in step 5 below. For manufacturing workspaces, full inventory workflows are covered in Raw Materials & Inventory.

3. Finished Goods Stock (for Production) ​

If starting production from zero with no finished goods on shelves yet, skip this item. To put already finished products from prior accounting onto stock without running simulated production batches:

  1. Open the "Finished Goods" tab and click "Receive Goods" (or "+" in the toolbar).
  2. In "Select Product", keep "— New product (enter name) —" (or choose an existing model) and enter "Product Name".
  3. Specify "Quantity (pcs)", historical "Unit Cost", and optionally "Retail Price (RRP, optional)".
  4. Click "Receive"—stock enters inventory as a FIFO batch with zero raw material consumption and zero cash expense.

4. Service Price List & Client Directory (for Services) ​

If you enabled "Services and Maintenance", populating your baseline directories before the first order keeps job entry fast:

  • Service Catalog: on the "Services" tab, click "Add Service", enter "Service Name" and "Fixed Price" (plus notes if needed), and click "Save".
  • Clients: on the "Clients" tab, add recurring customers via "New Client" ("Client Name", "Phone", and "Notes").

5. Opening Cash Balance ​

Recording equipment purchases (when "Without tracking in Cash Flow" is unchecked) or bringing in raw stock via "Batch Purchase" creates expense entries so "Cash Flow" may start negative—this reflects capital tied up in workshop inventory. To record actual cash or bank balances on hand at start (including offsetting pre-paid shelf stock):

  1. Open the "Cash Flow" tab.
  2. Under "Enter Other Transactions", select "Transaction Type" ➔ "Non-operating Income" and "Category" ➔ "Other".
  3. Enter your opening "Amount" (with an optional comment) and click "Add Transaction".

Built-in Migration Assistant

You can open the interactive 4-step migration checklist anytime from the top menu: "Help ➔ Migration & Initial Balances...".

Step 5. First Practical Operation and Verification ​

With equipment, categorized materials, and defaults in place, follow the workflow for your workspace profile.

Manufacturing and Craft: Costing, Running & Completing a Batch ​

  1. Open the "Calculator" tab, switch to "Recipe Database", and click "Create Recipe".
  2. In "Recipe Builder" under "Product Materials", enter "Product name (recipe)", specify "Labor hours (h)", pick the "Material" added in Step 4 (optionally filtering the list by "Category"), enter unit "Quantity", and click "Add" (at least one material is required; optionally configure packaging under "Packaging Assembly"). Click "Save".
  3. Switch back to "Batch Calculator", choose your recipe in "Find product recipe", and enter "Quantity (pcs)" (plus an optional selection under "Packaging").
  4. The right-hand "BATCH COST DETAILS" panel breaks down materials, master labor, overhead costs, packaging configuration, and displays "TOTAL COST".
Batch Cost Formula
How OmniCraft ERP Computes Total Cost
Total Cost=Materials+(Labor Hours×Hourly Rate)+Overheads+Packaging

Packaging is added when a packaging set is selected for the run. Recommended retail price (RRP) derived from your markup appears on the finished product card in "Finished Goods" once production is completed.

Click "Start Production". When stock is sufficient, raw materials are reserved immediately and the batch is queued on the "Production" tab (if shelf stock is short, an "Insufficient Materials!" warning lets you queue it anyway via "Yes, Plan"). Once assembly finishes, click "Complete" on the order row in "Production"—reserved stock is deducted, and finished items with their calculated RRP move to "Finished Goods", ready for sale under "Sales". See Calculator (Recipes) and Production (Workbench) for deeper workflows.

Services and Maintenance: Creating and Closing a Service Order ​

  1. Open the "Orders" tab and click "New Service Order".
  2. Fill in "Order Description", enter the customer name in "Client", specify "Total Amount", and enter any deposit under "Prepayment (Advance)".
  3. Click "Save". The card appears in the list with status NEW, and any advance payment immediately updates the ledger on the "Cash Flow" tab.
  4. Once the job is finished, click the complete icon ("Complete Order") on the order card—the system automatically posts the final settlement to "Cash Flow".

How to Verify Everything Worked ​

  • In "Production and Craft": "Raw Materials" lists items with real stock quantities and MAC unit prices, "Calculator" computes full batch cost with non-zero materials, and "Production" shows your queued batch (and clicking "Complete" moves finished units with RRP to "Finished Goods").
  • In "Services and Maintenance": your tools and consumables are recorded under "Assets" and "Materials", the order appears on "Orders", and "Cash Flow" reflects both your opening balance and any order advance or final payment.

First-Launch FAQ ​

Where is my data stored and how do I move it to another PC?

Your database omnicraft_erp.db, isolated workspaces/ files, and automatic backups reside locally inside %APPDATA%\OmniCraft. To transfer accounting to another computer, choose "File ➔ Export Data...", save the archive, and restore it on the new machine via "File ➔ Import Data...". Full details are covered in Backups & Recovery.

What if I picked the wrong workspace during setup?

Open the workspaces drawer in the top-right corner of the window, archive the current workspace, and create the one you need. On Free and Standard tiers, accidental archiving can be undone at no cost within 60 minutes.

How do I change the interface language or accounting currency?

The interface language and theme can be changed at any time via "File ➔ Program Settings" (on the "General" tab).

Accounting currency is configured in the same window. We recommend selecting your preferred currency before entering initial records (materials, equipment, inventory, or cash transactions). Once workspace records exist, the currency symbol is locked to maintain the mathematical accuracy of accumulated financial reports and unit costs. If you need a different currency later, you can create a dedicated workspace via the workspaces drawer or reset initial test data. For more details, see the Global Settings guide.
