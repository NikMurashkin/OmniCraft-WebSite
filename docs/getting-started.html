Configuring your workshop requires only four quick, intuitive steps:

Step 1. Installing the Application ​

The installation is engineered to be friction-free:

  • User-Level Installation without Administrator Privileges: The application installs into your personal user directory %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\OmniCraft ERP. The installer never requests elevated administrative credentials (Windows UAC), preventing security blocks and allowing seamless, secure in-place updates.
  • 1-Click Experience: Simply click the prominent "Install" button on the setup screen. Files extract automatically, a Desktop shortcut is generated, and you are prompted to open the app right away.
  • Advanced Settings: If you need to customize the installation directory or decline the Desktop shortcut, click the "Installation settings" link at the bottom of the installer dialog.

Step 2. First Launch: Selecting an Accounting Workspace ​

Upon first launch, the welcome screen prompts you to choose the primary workspace tailored to your craft:

  • "Production and Craft" (button Enable Production): designed for leathercraft, woodworking, jewelry, sewing, ceramic studios, and 3D printing labs. Features raw material inventory with moving average cost calculation, batch job costing, production workbench, and finished goods stock with batch tracking.
  • "Services and Maintenance" (button Enable Services): tailored for repair centers, electronics maintenance, cobblers, detailing shops, laser cutting, and engraving. Includes a client database, work orders with multi-stage progress tracking and customer deposits, master labor tariffs, and consumable deductions.

Live Demo Mode: "Try on a live example"

If you prefer to explore system features using realistic pre-filled data, click "Try on a live example". Clicking "Fill workspaces" immediately loads sample raw materials, recipes, and orders so you can test job costing and deductions without manual data entry.

Workspace Policy by Edition

  • Free and Standard Licenses: support 1 active workspace. You can archive the current workspace at any time to open another (with a 60-minute undo window for accidental archiving).
  • Professional Edition (PRO): unlocks simultaneous multi-space operations with instant tab switching and permanent archive access.

After activating a workspace, you arrive at the main desktop. Empty sections greet you with helpful quick-start triggers (such as "+ Add first material" on the raw inventory shelf), allowing you to begin without studying lengthy documentation.

Step 3. Migrating Data from Previous Records (in 15 Minutes) ​

When migrating from paper notebooks, spreadsheets, or legacy accounting packages, do not spend days back-filling years of past historical sales. For managerial control, you only need an accurate snapshot of your business as of today across three simple actions:

1. Record Current Assets and Materials ​

  1. Open the Workshop tab and enter your active machines, power tools, and hand equipment at their original acquisition cost.
  2. Open the Materials tab and log the physical raw materials (leather, timber, chemicals, hardware, clay) currently on your workshop shelves.

System outcome: These assets populate your Workshop Balance Sheet. Concurrently, the system records the acquisition expenses in your Cash Flow Ledger. At this stage, your cash balance logically moves into a negative figure—transparently accounting for your historical capital investment in workshop tools and inventory.

2. Receive Finished Goods into Stock ​

If you already have finished goods ready for dispatch or displayed in your showroom, there is no need to back-calculate complex recipes or trigger artificial production orders:

  1. Open the Finished Goods tab (under the "Production" section).
  2. Click the "+" ("Receive Goods") action button in the upper-left corner (or in the center of an empty screen).
  3. In the dialog, provide:
    • Product Name (create a new product or pick from existing);
    • Quantity (pcs) — physical on-hand balance;
    • Unit Cost — calculated historical manufacturing cost per unit from your previous records;
    • Retail Price (optional — for online showcase and fast checkout).
  4. Click "Receive".

System outcome: Products immediately enter inventory under a dedicated FIFO batch. No raw materials are consumed from storage, and no cash outflow occurs in Cash Flow—items are immediately ready for sale with accurate gross profit tracking.

3. Record Initial Finances (Cash Flow & Capital) ​

OmniCraft ERP operates under a mixed finances paradigm by default. A negative cash balance at the start is completely natural: it transparently demonstrates the total historical capital invested in machinery and materials during Step 1. As finished items are sold, customer revenue replenishes the cash drawer, moving the balance into profit and proving capital payback.

If you prefer to separate personal savings from workshop cash:

  1. Assets purchased long ago from personal savings: When adding equipment in the Workshop tab, toggle "Without Cash Flow expense". Equipment values will be placed under Capital without triggering a deduction in Cash Flow.
  2. Physical cash on hand or in a workshop bank account: If you already maintain a dedicated business balance (e.g., $2,000), open the Cash Flow tab and select "Record Other Transactions":
    • Transaction Type: Non-Operating Income;
    • Category: Other;
    • Amount: Your physical starting cash balance;
    • Comment: "Starting cash balance upon program setup".

Your database is now configured: the Balance Sheet and Cash Flow Ledger reflect reality without accounting complexity.

Step 4. Core Pricing and Costing Rules ​

Before creating items in the Calculator tab, configure your workshop financial guidelines. Open the Settings tab and establish three foundational values:

1. Master Hourly Rate (Currency/Hour) ​

The direct labor valuation for one hour of production work:

  • If you employ assistants or apprentices: Enter their average hourly labor rate. The software treats this as a direct production expense and allocates it to batch cost.
  • If you operate solo: You can leave the rate at 0. This eliminates the need for manual salary disbursements, but remember that the value of your labor must be built into your global retail markup.

2. Global Markup (%) ​

Your gross profit margin multiplier. The system automatically applies this percentage to the total unit cost (materials + labor + overheads) to determine your Recommended Retail Price.

3. Workshop Overhead Norms ​

Indirect operating expenditures: studio rent, heating, electricity, tooling wear, and workshop consumables. They can be configured as:

  • Percentage (%): Calculated proportionally to raw material and labor expenses.
  • Fixed (Currency): A flat monetary charge attributed to each produced unit.

Fine-Tuning Coefficients per Product

You never need to alter global parameters when building products of varying complexity. Within each recipe calculator, a multiplier slider is available:

  • If a complex briefcase requires twice as much machine run-time and specialty glue, set the overhead multiplier to 2.0.
  • If a simple keychain uses no heavy machinery, set the multiplier to 0 to omit overhead allocations.

Language & Data Security ​

Localization ​

The application supports multiple languages and automatically detects your operating system language upon initial launch. You can change the language anytime via File ➔ Program Settings and restart the app.

Data Privacy & Sovereignty ​

Your commercial records remain strictly your private property:

  • The application runs 100% locally and offline on your computer;
  • We do not collect, transmit, or store your customer names, sales figures, or pricing;
  • No account registration, email submission, or phone verification is ever required.

Failure Resilience and Backups

The database runs on an embedded SQLite engine configured with Write-Ahead Logging (WAL), safeguarding your data against file corruption during sudden power losses or OS crashes.

Create regular backup snapshots via File ➔ Export Data:

  1. Primary Binary Backup (.db): compresses the entire database state into a single archive in one click.
  2. Safety Fallback Backup («Active Workspace Tables (.zip with CSV)»): exports raw data into human-readable CSV spreadsheets, ensuring complete accessibility even in extreme hardware failure scenarios.
