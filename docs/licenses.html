Licenses & Limitations: Fair Trial, Licensing Tiers, and Offline Security ​

For an artisan, service technician, or boutique workshop owner, software is an essential work tool—not an excuse for unpredictable recurring monthly fees. If a cloud vendor decides to alter their pricing, suspend an account, or suffer an outage, the business risks immediate loss of access to its customer records and financial ledgers.

OmniCraft ERP is built on a fundamentally different architectural model:

  • Local Data Sovereignty: the software operates as a self-contained desktop application for Windows. The database resides strictly on your physical machine, guaranteeing complete privacy for your operational trade secrets;
  • Unrestricted Fair Trial: the evaluation version never disables planning tools, hides inventory sheets, or locks balance reports. You receive a fully functional system for testing under genuine workshop conditions;
  • Transparent Growth Path: a commercial license lifts the single trial limit and unlocks multi-business scaling as your trade expands.

Workspace Architecture: Licensing Guidelines ​

OmniCraft ERP enables flexible workspace configuration ("Production" or "Services") right from the initial cold start:

  • Single Active Workspace on Free and Standard Tiers: designed for managing one complete enterprise (such as a leathercraft studio or watch repair center).
  • Industry Pivots via Archive: if your trade focus changes or you wish to start accounting fresh, the active enterprise is moved to the archive, freeing the slot to create a new workspace.
  • 15-Minute Accidental Archive Safety Window: clicking the archive button triggers a 15-minute grace period where you can restore the workspace to active status immediately free of charge.
  • Why Unarchiving Requires PRO: unrestricted unarchiving on the Standard tier would allow serial rotation of dozens of separate businesses under a single entry license. Running multiple simultaneous or alternating operations is specifically supported under the PRO license.
  • Complete Data Liberation: database backups (.db, .zip) and CSV table exports remain freely accessible and downloadable from archived spaces at any time without paying for a license.

How the Free Limit Works (20 Sales Transactions) ​

The evaluation version of OmniCraft ERP differs sharply from typical crippled demo software. Every calculation model, recipe sheet, material consumption norm, and inventory report remains fully accessible.

The only boundary is placed at the very end of the production pipeline—the moment payment is registered from a client.

What Happens When the Limit Is Reached ​

When the processed sales counter in the "Sales" tab reaches 20 transactions, the software automatically switches the sales recording engine to read-only protection:

  • Blocked Checkout Operations: the "Process Sale" button in the POS terminal and the "Complete Production" button on the workbench become disabled. A top banner appears inviting you to activate a commercial license;
  • Unrestricted Inspection and Planning: you can freely review analytics, add materials, craft new recipe bills of materials, evaluate margin charts, and export backups;
  • Zero Historical Data Loss: no data is erased. Entering a commercial key immediately unlocks the sales register with all past records preserved.

Economic Safeguards Against Trial Tampering ​

In typical software, users attempt to reset trials by clearing historical tables. In OmniCraft ERP, doing so is economically self-defeating.

The entire value of management accounting lies in continuous, unbroken historical records. If an artisan manually deletes sales transactions to reset the counter, the system experiences structural disconnections that break:

  1. The Management Balance Sheet ("Capital"): distortion of equity calculations and net worth metrics;
  2. Cash Flow Records ("Cash Flow / DDS"): real bank and cash drawer balances diverge from the software register;
  3. FIFO Batch Cost Accounting: inventory valuations of remaining finished stock become inaccurate;
  4. Profitability Analytics: revenue history and break-even point charts are wiped clean.

Consequently, craftsmen are personally invested in the integrity and continuity of their operational database. Purchasing a license is not merely a software unlock, but an investment in protecting the digital foundation of your craft.

Offline Cryptographic Security and Privacy (Zero-PII) ​

OmniCraft ERP is a local-first desktop application. It never transmits operational ledgers or financial transactions to remote servers. License verification is executed 100% autonomously without continuous network connectivity.

1. Computer Hardware Signature ​

Upon initial launch, the program computes a hardware device fingerprint using motherboard parameters and operating system registry entries:

  • Sub-Millisecond Execution: hardware signature calculation requires under 0.1 milliseconds and never delays application startup;
  • Complete Anonymity: the signature is a masked hexadecimal string (e.g., A1B2-C3D4-E5F6-G7H8). It contains no account names, emails, passwords, network addresses, or filenames;
  • Privacy Compliance: the billing architecture eliminates the gathering, storage, or processing of personal data (fully GDPR/CCPA compliant).

2. Asymmetric Public-Key Cryptography ​

To prevent tampering and key counterfeiting, asymmetric public-key cryptography protects the system:

  • Embedded Public Key: compiled directly into the application binary. It is mathematically capable only of verifying digital signatures, never generating them;
  • Isolated Private Key: stored securely on the isolated licensing infrastructure and never distributed with client binaries.

3. Anonymous License Tokens ​

Purchasing a license requires no intrusive registration forms or phone verifications. The billing server generates an anonymous token formatted as LIC-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX, linking only the license tier and a list of approved hardware hashes.

Every commercial license allows simultaneous activation on up to 5 computers (e.g., workshop desktop, laptop, and front-desk order terminal).

Step-by-Step Guide: Activating Your License ​

The activation procedure takes less than one minute and requires zero technical background:

  • Step 1: Copy Your Hardware Signature. Open "Settings" from the application menu. Locate your hardware signature in the "License Management" card and click the copy icon.
  • Step 2: Obtain Your Key or Token. During checkout on the website, paste your hardware signature into the order form. You will receive an anonymous license token or an encoded text license key.
  • Step 3: Activate the Software. Paste your key or token into the input field in Settings and click the "Activate" button.

Dynamic Window Title Status

Activating your key requires no application restart. The Windows title bar updates dynamically: changing from the trial badge to the active commercial tier: OmniCraft ERP (Standard) or OmniCraft ERP (PRO), alongside the count of active trade extensions.

Workshop Frequently Asked Questions ​

Is an active internet connection required to verify the license? ​

No. Cryptographic key verification is performed locally by the embedded security engine on your PC. An internet connection is used only during the initial 1-second online token binding. All subsequent day-to-day operations run 100% offline.

What happens to my historical records when a subscription expires? ​

All your databases, bills of materials, warehouse stock levels, and transaction histories remain completely intact on your local drive. The software never restricts access to historical data or deletes records. If a subscription period concludes, the app simply switches the POS checkout into read-only mode until renewed.

On how many machines can I use a single license? ​

Each commercial license includes 5 distinct hardware slots. You can install the application on your workshop desktop, mobile laptop, and home PC. If a computer is replaced, unbind the old slot in the "Devices" dialog and activate your new machine.

How do I upgrade from Standard to PRO? ​

You never need to repurchase the software at full price. In the License Settings card, click the "Upgrade to PRO" button: a checkout portal opens with automatic credit for your existing tier, allowing you to pay only the price difference.
