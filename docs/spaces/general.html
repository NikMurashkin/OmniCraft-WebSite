Workspaces Overview ​

At the heart of the OmniCraft ERP architecture lies the principle of strict workspace isolation. An experienced craftsman, artisan, or workshop manager rarely limits operations to a single narrow craft: woodworking often coexists with bespoke restoration, leathercraft with repair services, and commercial manufacturing with personal creative projects.

Attempting to run such diverse endeavors inside a single ledger inevitably leads to distorted cost calculations, raw material stock confusion, and cash register discrepancies. In OmniCraft ERP, each operational direction lives its own economic life, preserving absolute ledger clarity.

Multi-Tenancy Architecture & Isolation ​

Every workspace in OmniCraft ERP operates as a fully autonomous digital workshop powered by its own SQLite database:

  • Physical File Isolation: Each workspace database is stored in an independent database file within the local application folder. Operations, archiving, or parameter adjustments in one workshop are physically isolated from neighboring projects.
  • Independent Cost Economics: Every workspace maintains dedicated standards: craftsman hourly rates, target commercial markups, and specific overhead allocations.
  • Separated Warehouses and Cash Flow: Raw material inventories, machinery assets, cash flow entries, and customer accounts are strictly compartmentalized, preventing manufacturing purchases from blending into repair parts.
  • Zero Cloud Dependencies: All databases remain stored locally on your machine. The software never transmits proprietary production data to third-party servers and operates seamlessly offline.

Protecting Financial Clarity

Separating distinct ventures into dedicated workspaces protects you from false illusions of profitability. You can always pinpoint exactly which direction generates net cash and which requires recipe cost optimization or rate adjustments.

First Run: Onboarding Walkthrough ​

Upon launching a clean installation of the application, an onboarding window welcomes you to select the initial profile for your workshop:

  1. Selecting the Core Direction: You choose the foundational model—"Production and Craft" or "Services and Maintenance". The application instantly arranges the relevant tabs and terminology to match your operational reality.
  2. Interactive Demonstration Mode: At the bottom of the onboarding screen, the "Try on a live example" action is available. Selecting this option populates the workspace with realistic demonstration data (sample materials, product recipes, machines, and trial transactions). This allows you to inspect system mechanics immediately without entering inventory from scratch.

Safe Test Drive

Demonstration data serves as an interactive workshop handbook. You can test-launch batch production runs on the workbench, process sales, and deduct raw materials with zero risk. Once you are ready to track real operations, create a clean workspace or clear the database with a single click.

Active Industry Profiles ​

Current versions of OmniCraft ERP feature two core operational profiles:

1. Production & Craft Profile ​

Engineered for classic physical manufacturing where purchased raw resources undergo processing, cutting, and assembly:

  • Raw Materials Warehouse: Material intake and deductions with automatic cost valuation using the Moving Average Cost (MAC) method.
  • Recipe Cost Calculator: Precise tracking of material yields, labor hours, and overhead allocations per finished unit to determine accurate breakeven points.
  • Production Workbench: Batch queue tracking, raw material reservation, and completed batch release.
  • Finished Goods & Retail Sales: Inventory tracking of completed items and sales logging with First-In, First-Out (FIFO) lot deduction.

2. Services & Repair Center Profile ​

Tailored for service centers, custom tailoring, tool sharpening, electronics repair, and bespoke custom projects:

  • Services Catalog: Price list supporting fixed pricing per operation, standard hourly rates, and compound estimates.
  • Work Orders Workbench: Stage-by-stage job tracking, deduction of parts and consumables, advance deposit logging, and balance due calculations.
  • Client Database: Customer contact directory with complete historical visit logs. Walk-in jobs can be processed in seconds without mandatory customer registration.
  • Direct Cash Flow Integration: Completing a work order automatically logs payments directly into the cash flow ledger (DDS), eliminating duplicate manual entries.
Architectural Extensibility

The workspace creation dialog also displays prospective domain templates ("Trade & Retail", "Education & Digital", "Activities & Hobbies"). These are marked with a lock badge, reflecting our core architectural roadmap for upcoming platform expansions.

Drawer Workspace Management ​

All workspace operations are centrally accessible via the compact sliding side drawer, summoned by clicking the workshop icon (⛯) in the application header:

  • Workshop Identity: Each workspace holds a distinct custom title ("Heritage Leathercraft", "Precision Optics Repair") and a detailed description (workshop address, working hours, or core specializations).
  • Clean Two-Line Preview: The workspace card displays up to two lines of description with clean ellipsis truncation. The interface remains lightweight and free of intrusive hover popups.
  • Editing Parameters: Clicking the pencil icon opens an inspection window to review full descriptive notes and adjust records.
  • Rapid Switching: Clicking any workspace card instantly shifts the active operational context to that workshop.

Workspace Lifecycle & Tier Rules ​

Managing workspaces follows strict reliability guidelines that prevent accidental data loss:

One Active Workspace Policy (Free & Standard Tiers) ​

  • Focused Execution: Base licenses keep strictly one active workspace open at a time, ensuring total focus on the primary venture. You operate either a production facility or a repair center.
  • Flexible Reorientation via Archive: If you transition your craft or launch a new venture, the current space can be archived through the side drawer at any moment. This frees the active slot to launch a new workspace.

60-Minute Grace Period Undo ​

  • Accidental Action Safeguard: When sending a workspace to the archive, a 60-minute grace timer begins in the side drawer.
  • Instant Free Restoration: Within 60 minutes, you can restore the archived space back to active status with a single click at no cost.
  • Post-60-Minute Policy: After one hour, unarchiving requires a PRO license. This invariant protects the software from serial multi-business abuse on single-business licenses.

Multi-Workspaces in PRO Tier ​

The PRO license removes operational boundaries:

  • Unlimited Active Workspaces: Simultaneously manage dozens of workshops, studios, and branches.
  • Instant Switching: Jump between concurrent enterprises in one second without archiving.
  • Unrestricted Restoration: Unarchive historical databases at any time.

Data Liberation Invariant (Zero Lock-In) ​

OmniCraft ERP never holds your records hostage. Even when a workspace is archived, you can always export complete database backups and CSV spreadsheets free of charge.

Capital Consolidation: Connection with Core Ledger ​

Dividing workshops into isolated spaces does not fragment the overarching visibility of your net wealth.

At the highest echelon of the system sits the "My Capital" module. It automatically queries databases across all your active workshops, aggregating active machinery valuation, raw material warehouse reserves, finished stock inventory, and liquid cash balances.

This delivers a unified consolidated balance sheet and calculates your true net worth without tedious manual spreadsheet reconciliation. For an in-depth breakdown of cross-cutting ledger mechanics, explore the Capital documentation guide.
