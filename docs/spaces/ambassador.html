Ambassador Module: Business Promotion and Partner Ecosystem ​

Every craftsman crafting quality goods or delivering dependable repair services eventually faces the challenge of customer acquisition. Some publish masterclasses and tips on specialized forums, others build social media communities, while others invest in paid advertising or collaborate with fellow artisans.

In most small business applications, marketing workflows are either completely omitted or outsourced to complex, expensive external software. In OmniCraft ERP, this capability is provided as a complimentary built-in extension — the Ambassador module.

The tool solves two critical objectives for working entrepreneurs:

  • Promoting Your Own Workshop: systematic discovery of promotional channels, advertising campaign management, deterministic tracking link generation, AI-assisted content drafting, and transparent return-on-investment analytics;
  • Affiliate Revenue in the OmniCraft ERP Ecosystem: introducing the platform to fellow artisans while earning lifetime commissions (up to 50% on every payment from referred users), with flexible payouts to bank accounts or direct license redemptions.

Tool Architecture: Dual-Mode Operation and Data Isolation ​

The module is engineered for complete operational autonomy and minimal computer resource footprint:

CabinetSourcesCampaignsAnalyticsSettings
Partner Profile & LinksChannel Directory & AIActive Campaigns & CopyFunnels & ROI ReportsAutomation & API
  • Dual-Mode Execution: the module operates as an integrated screen within the primary OmniCraft ERP workspace (matching the unified command bar aesthetic of "My Capital"), and can also be launched as a standalone executable Ambassador.exe.
  • Global Data Preservation: all generated links, channel records, draft publications, and metrics are stored in a dedicated secure database file global_extensions.db. If you reconfigure your workshop, purge test cash registers, or archive a production workspace, the module's records remain untouched.
  • Network Disconnection Resilience: link creation, directory updates, and local metric evaluations operate 100% offline. During temporary internet drops, the system instantly serves cached data without application freezes.

Direct Access via Menu

Access the module at any time via the main application drawer or the "Cross-Cutting Modules" sidebar group. The tool is available free of charge across all commercial licenses.

Quick Link Generator ​

When you need to send a targeted link to a colleague in direct messages or share a cost calculator in a discussion thread, there is no need to set up a comprehensive campaign.

The "Quick Link" generator requires only three straightforward inputs:

  • Link Name: a descriptive label for your records (e.g., "Leathercraft Discord Discussion");
  • Target Page: select the destination URL from the built-in catalog:
    • Home Page (/);
    • Product Cost Calculator (/calculator);
    • Pricing & Margins (/pricing);
    • Software Overview (/software);
    • Knowledge Base & Blog (/blog);
    • Interactive Web Demo (/demo);
    • Custom Destination URL (any valid URL on the official domain);
  • Campaign Tag and Notes: optional fields for enhanced bookkeeping.

Click the blue "Create Link" button: the system normalizes the path, appends your unique referral code, and renders the finished URL ready for one-click clipboard copying.

Unified Links Center (Directory) ​

Below the generator sits the master directory of all created links with intuitive filters:

  • "All Links" — master overview of every active tracking link;
  • "Quick Links" — standalone links created for direct messaging;
  • "From Campaigns" — links explicitly associated with promotional projects.

Each entry provides four key interactions:

  1. Copy: instant clipboard copying;
  2. QR Code Inspection: modal window displaying a high-contrast QR code optimized for smartphone scanning directly off desktop displays;
  3. Campaign Association: retroactively attach any standalone quick link to a campaign;
  4. Safe Removal: purge outdated links with explicit confirmation.

Organic Growth: QR Codes on Electronic Sales Receipts ​

One of the most effective organic channels is sharing software credentials directly with real customers of your workshop.

Enable the "Place QR code on receipts" toggle:

  • When completing a retail order in the POS register, OmniCraft ERP automatically attaches a clean QR code badge to the bottom of digital receipts;
  • Customers scan the code from their mobile device, visit the site, and explore the system;
  • If the recipient ever licenses OmniCraft ERP for their own business, the billing server permanently binds the account to your partner profile.

The right side of the card displays a live preview showing how the receipt badge renders on paper and mobile screens.

Anatomy of a Channel Record ​

Every promotional source is cataloged across six distinct classifications:

  • Community: social media groups, channels, and workshop chat groups;
  • Article / Blog: guest editorial posts, craft industry portals, and publications;
  • Forum: traditional discussion boards with threaded topics;
  • Video: video hosting platforms, equipment reviews, and tutorial channels;
  • Advertising: sponsored banners, search placements, and direct promotions;
  • Other: trade fairs, craft exhibitions, and in-person meetups.

Beyond type, each card tracks audience demographics, moderation policies (to protect against spam violations), preferred communication tone, and the cooldown interval between subsequent posts in days.

User-Managed Verified Channel Library (Zero-Hardcode) ​

OmniCraft ERP includes no hardcoded promotional directories. The library is curated entirely by the craftsman:

  • Any record can be edited, updated with administrator contacts, or archived;
  • The directory displays participation counts (e.g., "Leathercraft Club: 3 campaigns, 2 active");
  • Complete functionality is available in fully manual mode — even without configuring external AI credentials, you can add channels using the standard "Add Source" button.

The Campaign Lifecycle ​

Campaigns are created for concrete workshop milestones: showcasing a new custom toolset, publishing an apprenticeship curriculum, or launching a costing calculator.

The workflow follows five clear steps:

  • Step 1: Create Campaign. Click "New Campaign", assign a clear title, select the landing page destination, configure execution dates, and add categorical tags for reporting.
  • Step 2: Assign Channels. Connect verified sources from your library. For each placement, specify the mode ("Organic" or "Paid" with dedicated budget figures).
  • Step 3: Deterministic Link Generation. When a channel is attached, the system automatically builds an exact "Campaign × Source" pair with unique parameters. All links are generated by code, eliminating human formatting errors.
  • Step 4: Draft Publication Copy. Compose copy tailored to the platform's format (forum technical response, community post, long-form editorial), or write copy manually.
  • Step 5: Publish and Track Progress. Place the material and update the status to Published. The system triggers the follow-up countdown.

Participation Lifecycle Statuses ​

Each placement progresses across four distinct stages:

  • Candidate — source is tentatively selected, but publication terms are not yet finalized;
  • In Progress — copy is being drafted or reviewed with platform administrators;
  • Published — content is live on the target site with an active tracking link;
  • Completed — promotion period concluded, with performance captured in analytics.

Deterministic Link Injection ​

When drafting promotional content using AI, OmniCraft ERP enforces an unbreakable engineering invariant: the language model generates strictly placeholder tokens and .

The actual target URL containing your affiliate identifier and channel parameters is injected programmatically by application code right before rendering. The neural model is physically incapable of distorting or losing tracking tokens.

"Next Step" Automation ​

Upon setting an item to Published, the software schedules future operational checkpoints:

  1. At 3 Days: reminder notification to check initial clicks and sign-ups in Analytics;
  2. Post-Cooldown: notification indicating the channel cooldown has expired, allowing safe follow-up posting without risking community moderation penalties.

5. Autonomous Digital Assistants and Model Context Protocol ​

The Ambassador module is built for modern automation workflows, featuring out-of-the-box support for the open Model Context Protocol (MCP).

This enables developers and technical craftsmen to connect local autonomous agents (e.g., inside Cursor, Antigravity, or Claude Desktop) directly to marketing operations:

  • Sandboxed Tool Registry: assistants interact via controlled API endpoints:
    • list_campaigns — retrieve active campaign registries;
    • get_stats — query real-time performance metrics;
    • create_link — programmatically generate tracking links;
    • generate_content — request platform-specific copy drafts;
    • mark_published — confirm live publication status;
    • export_task — export full task context payloads.
  • Autonomy Levels and Human-in-the-Loop Safeguards:
    • Draft: the assistant only drafts text variants and awaits human approval;
    • Semi-Auto: automated posting that pauses immediately when CAPTCHA challenges or two-factor verifications appear;
    • Autopilot: strictly limited to explicitly trusted internal channels.
  • Audit Logging: every tool execution is permanently captured in an audit table along with response times and submitted parameters.

Workshop Frequently Asked Questions ​

Is an external AI API key mandatory to use the module? ​

No, it is entirely optional. The module is architected so that all primary workflows — manual link building, source directory management, ad spend tracking, receipt QR codes, conversion funnels, and analytics — function completely without external neural models. AI serves strictly as an optional workflow accelerator.

How are commissions credited if a user visits the link but buys weeks later? ​

When a visitor arrives through your link, their browser receives an attribution cookie. When that artisan eventually purchases a license weeks later, the billing server connects the transaction to your referral code and credits your balance.

What happens to links and metrics when switching or archiving workspaces? ​

Nothing is lost. The Ambassador module is a cross-cutting global tool. Its data repository is isolated from individual trade workspaces. You can switch industries, clear test orders, or create new workshops — all tracking links, campaign archives, and earned balances remain intact.

How do I prevent spam complaints when sharing links on community forums? ​

The core principle of ethical marketing is providing genuine utility. Share links only in the context of solving a fellow artisan's concrete question (such as sharing a completed recipe cost breakdown or a pattern allowance guide). In every channel record, maintain reasonable cooldown periods and heed the "Next Step" scheduling reminders.
