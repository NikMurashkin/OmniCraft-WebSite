Craft Web Store & Showcase ​

The built-in Web Store module transforms your workshop recipe catalog, warehouse inventory, and product photography into an autonomous, lightning-fast static website without recurring subscription fees for proprietary site builders and without vulnerable server CMS platforms.

The showcase is compiled using static site generation (Jamstack SSG): the output consists of pure HTML, clean CSS, vanilla JavaScript, and optimized web-ready images. The generated website opens instantly on desktop and mobile devices, requires no database setup on your hosting server, and can be hosted for free on GitHub Pages, S3-compatible cloud storage, or standard web hosting via FTP.

Independence from Third-Party Clouds

Unlike cloud e-commerce platforms, the OmniCraft ERP showcase cannot be disabled by platform bans, incurs no recurring monthly costs, and embeds no third-party tracking scripts. All master data resides securely on your personal computer, and the published website belongs entirely to you.

Showcase Architecture and Layout ​

1. Home Storefront and Triptych Hero ​

The home page introduces visitors to the workshop and highlights signature pieces:

  • Triptych Hero Section: combines the workshop value proposition, featured piece photography, and three value cards (e.g., traditional saddle stitch, vegetable-tanned leather, lifetime craftsmanship guarantee).
  • Curated Work Showcase: highlighted pieces with distinct status badges ("Hit", "New", "Custom Order", "In Stock").
  • Master's Word Block: personal craftsman column featuring author portrait photography, philosophy quote, and signature to establish direct client trust.

2. Product Page and Quiet Luxury Architecture ​

The product detail page delivers a benchmark presentation experience for bespoke craftsmanship:

  • 3D Triptych Gallery Slider (Cinematic Hero Gallery): full-width spatial gallery with real CSS depth (perspective: 1200px). The active center slide is flanked by angled preview cards at ±15° with smooth 3D translations, creating a physical triptych feel. Full touch-swipe support on mobile devices, arrow navigation, and dot indicators.
  • Floating Hero Overlay: sits elegantly over the lower edge of the slider. Displays the product title, price, "DETAILS ⌄" smooth anchor button, and "Other Colors" trigger that opens a variant picker modal (3:4 aspect ratio cards with active badge).
  • Adaptive Scrolled Mode Header: floating transparent navbar over the dark hero that smoothly transitions to an opaque frosted white background with subtle elevation shadow (is-scrolled-mode), fading in a centered product title, "MENU" trigger, and pill-shaped Cart button.
  • Two-Column Detail Layout (#product-details):
    • Left Sticky Purchase Panel: remains fixed in view during scrolling (position: sticky; top: 100px;). Features a bold 36px price, 56px styled dropdown selectors with 9px tracking micro-labels, quantity stepper, monumental 64px CTA button ("ADD TO CART", 100px border radius), direct Telegram & WhatsApp consultation shortcuts, technical specs list, luxury tier badge, and delivery snippet.
    • Right Narrative & Storytelling Column: author editorial layout — intro paragraph with traditional drop cap, divider, key advantages with crisp square bullet points, craftsman quote block with vertical bar and Georgia serif italics, signature, and stacked modular content sections.
  • Lower Curated and Reference Sections:
    • "Other Products" Grid: 4-column responsive grid showcasing complementary masterworks with a centered "VIEW ALL PRODUCTS" button.
    • FAQ Accordion: interactive expandable drawers addressing leather care, hardware durability, and warranty terms.
  • Sticky Mobile Buy Bar: pinned to the bottom of smartphone screens directly under the thumb, enabling instant purchase without scrolling back to top.

The craftsman can freely arrange modular description blocks:

  • Lead Summary: emotional, high-contrast introductory statement.
  • Features Grid: clean icon cards detailing structural and ergonomic highlights.
  • Craftsman Story: narrative regarding leather selection, hand burnishing, or finishing.
  • Additional Gallery: atmospheric photo collection showcasing pieces in context, in use (lookbook), or inside the workshop, with easy batch uploading.
  • Video Showcase: lite-facade video adapter for VK, Rutube, or YouTube. External scripts do not load until the first play click, saving bandwidth and boosting page speed.
  • Specifications Table: structured technical matrix (dimensions, leather grade, hardware, weight).
  • FAQ Accordion: expandable drawers addressing maintenance advice and warranty terms.

3. Communication Channels, Checkout, and Privacy ​

The engine supports three independent checkout flows:

  1. Direct Messenger Chats: customer clicks the button and jumps directly into Telegram or WhatsApp with pre-filled line items and totals. Ideal for custom orders and personal touch.
  2. Autonomous Server Script (order.php): for standard PHP hosting, the compiler emits a lightweight handler with an invisible Honeypot trap, IP rate limiter, and direct Telegram Bot API dispatch.
  3. National Form Services: integrated support for Yandex Forms or Web3Forms for automated lead capture.

Privacy and Data Protection (152-FZ & GDPR)

The built-in legal page generator compiles customized Privacy Policy and Terms of Service documents with your business credentials and tax ID. The checkout drawer includes a mandatory consent checkbox referencing these policies.

Step-by-Step Deployment Guide ​

Step 1. Configure Profile and Sections ​

  1. Navigate to Web Store → subtab Profile & Styling.
  2. In the vertical sidebar, open Brand & Contacts:
    • Showcase Logo: upload a vector SVG, modern WebP / AVIF, or transparent PNG / JPG (includes instant live preview and one-click removal to revert to text branding).
    • Product Photo Backdrop: select a harmonious background color for catalog cards and the product 3D slider (presets: "Dark Graphite", "Light Neutral", "Pure White", "Warm Linen", or a custom HEX code), or upload a background texture image (supports WebP, AVIF, SVG, PNG, and JPG). The texture scales to fit the card and creates a stylish backdrop behind transparent and studio photos.
    • Store Name & Tagline: specify your brand name (defaults to universal "Online Store") and an optional tagline (omitted from the website when left empty).
    • Contacts & Story: enter your phone number, Telegram username (without @), WhatsApp number, contact email, city, and workshop story in Markdown format.
  3. In the Delivery Methods section, configure courier, pickup, and transport company shipping rates.
  4. In the Legal & 152-FZ section, enter business details and click Generate 152-FZ Templates.
  5. In the Checkout & Orders section, select your preferred ordering mode (direct messenger chat, standalone order.php, or Yandex Forms).
  6. In the Catalog, Blog & SEO section, configure blog visibility, currency symbol, and click Save Profile.

Step 2. Prepare Products for the Showcase ​

  1. Open the Showcase Catalog subtab.
  2. Select your preferred display mode in the right toolbar:
    • Showcase Grid 3:4: visual portrait cards showcasing items with 3:4 aspect ratio covers, marketing badges ("Hit", "New"), and an instant publication status overlay.
    • Compact Registry: dense tabular rows with thumbnails for quick inspection of extensive collections.
  3. Toggle the Publish switch for products that should appear online (accessible directly on the card cover in grid mode).
  4. Click the configuration button (slider icon) next to the item:
    • In General Properties, specify the URL slug, status badge, and retail web price.
    • In Photos, upload images, reorder them with ◄/► arrows, and select the card cover.
    • In Description Sections, add feature cards, specifications, or video demonstrations.
  5. Click Save Product Properties.

Step 3. Local Live Preview ​

Always verify your website locally prior to remote deployment:

  1. In the top showcase toolbar, click Live Preview (eye icon).
  2. The system compiles the showcase from the active database, starts an internal multithreaded web server, and opens the site in your default browser.
  3. Test dark/light theme switching, catalog filters, and order submission flows.

Strict Cache Policy

The preview server sends no-cache and must-revalidate HTTP headers, ensuring that edits saved in OmniCraft ERP appear immediately upon refreshing your browser (F5).

Step 4. Configure Transport and Deploy ​

  1. Open the Publishing Transport subtab.
  2. Select your hosting method:
    • Local Folder / ZIP: save the site bundle to local disk for manual distribution.
    • FTP / FTPS Protocol: direct upload to shared web hosting.
    • S3 Cloud Storage: upload to Yandex Cloud, VK Cloud, Selectel, MinIO, or AWS S3.
    • GitHub Pages: automatic deployment to the gh-pages branch using the official REST API.
  3. Click Test Connection. Credentials are encrypted using Windows DPAPI.
  4. Click Save Transport, then click Publish Website in the top toolbar. To access your deployed showcase, click Open Website (the button activates only after the first successful deployment in the active workspace; hovering over the disabled button displays an explanatory tooltip).

Order Inbox Workflow ​

All orders from the website and external channels arrive in the Order Inbox subtab:

  1. Website Orders: appear automatically with complete cart line items, customer details, and selected delivery method.
  2. Chat & Marketplace Inquiries: copy message text to clipboard and click Smart Paste. The system extracts customer name, phone number, shipping address, and desired products.
  3. Canned Responses: click Reply to select pre-configured templates ("Order Confirmed", "Ready to Ship", "Bank Details") with direct 1-click jump to Telegram or WhatsApp.
  4. 1-Click FIFO Stock Deduction: after shipping the package, click Complete Sale. The system automatically deducts finished goods from warehouse inventory using the FIFO method and logs revenue in Cash Flow (DDS).
