Service Catalog: Price List, Labor Hours & Pricing Models ​

In repair and maintenance workflows, an organized price list serves as your primary margin shield. When a workshop quotes prices off the top of the head or neglects reviewing legacy rates for years, it inevitably drifts into an economic trap: inflation erodes net receipts, premises rent and supplies climb, while complex technical jobs get billed at the price of trivial routine procedures.

In OmniCraft ERP, the service catalog functions as a flexible engineering tool. It allows you to partition your price list into clear-cut routine jobs with fixed tariffs and complex restorative tasks whose billing is derived directly from standardized technician labor hours and your workshop overhead factor.

Two Pricing Models for Workshop Services ​

The system supports two complementary calculation mechanisms. Every catalog entry in the registry is tagged with a distinct visual badge: "Fixed Price" or "By Labor Hours".

1. Fixed Tariff (fixed) ​

Intended for high-throughput routine operations with predictable scope and static execution steps:

  • Initial electronic diagnostics and component teardown;
  • Preventative cleaning and lubrication of mechanical assemblies;
  • Power cord replacement or standardized connector soldering;
  • Cutting tool sharpening and guide fence alignment.

In this model, the selling price is set statically within the service card and remains independent of fluctuations in the master hourly rate. This guarantees customers transparent and immediate upfront pricing.

2. Standardized Labor Hours (calculated) ​

Employed for non-standard repairs, delicate restorations, custom fitting, and complex overhauls where technician bench time represents the dominant cost driver.

The retail price is calculated dynamically according to the formula:

Pricing Formula
Standardized Labor Price Calculation
Final Price=(Labor Hours × Hourly Rate)×(1 + Workshop Overhead / 100)

Calculation Example: ​

If an intricate gearbox overhaul demands 2.0 hours at a base master rate of $12.00/h and a shop overhead allocation of 15%, the software determines the billing price: (2.0 × $12.00) × 1.15 = $27.60.

Master Craftsman Tip: Protecting Margin via Overhead Rates

Never constrain your billing to bench time alone. Throughout repairs, diamond burrs and soldering tips wear down, solvents and tapes are consumed, and diagnostic test benches consume electricity. Factoring in an overhead percentage automatically embeds equipment depreciation into every client invoice.

Dynamic System-Wide Revaluation via Settings ​

A foundational strength of OmniCraft ERP is eliminating the need to manually edit dozens of catalog cards whenever economic conditions shift in your workshop:

  1. Navigate to the "Settings" tab.
  2. In the workshop configuration block, update your "Master Hourly Rate" (e.g., advancing from $10.00/h to $12.00/h).
  3. Save your changes.

All catalog services tagged with the Calculated pricing model that rely on the general workshop rate instantly recalculate their selling tariffs. This enables a workshop owner to update an entire service catalog across the facility in five seconds.

Step-by-Step Guide: Adding a Service to the Catalog ​

Building your service registry takes place in the "Services" tab. The UI is designed for swift data entry on both desktop keyboards and touch screens.

  1. Click the "Add Service" button in the upper-right corner of the view.
  2. In the modal dialog, fill in the parameters:
    • "Service Name": Concise and unambiguous title easily understood by customers ("Rotary hammer brush gear replacement");
    • "Notes": Technical procedure breakdown or scope boundaries ("Includes housing disassembly, commutator blowing, and brush bedding");
    • "Fixed Price": The standard rate according to your workshop tariff (when using fixed pricing).
  3. Click the "Save" button.

The new entry immediately appears in your active list with its calculated selling price and pricing model badge.

Catalog Management: Search, Editing, and Soft Delete ​

As your service center matures, your operation list naturally expands into hundreds of specialized tasks. Dedicated utilities ensure effortless navigation:

  • Instant Search: Enter the initial letters of an operation in the search field at the top. The list filters on the fly. The cross icon on the right clears the search query in a single click.
  • Click the pencil icon on a service row to change its name, description, or fixed price.
  • Soft Delete: Clicking the trash icon triggers a confirmation dialog: "Are you sure you want to delete this service?".

What stays after deletion

The service disappears from the active catalog. Existing orders keep the line item names and prices recorded when the orders were created.

Cross-Module Integration Across the Workshop ​

The service catalog in OmniCraft ERP connects directly to every functional module:

  • Work Order Desk: When checking equipment into the shop, technicians pick tasks straight from the catalog. Standardized hours and prices populate automatically.
  • Online Storefront: Your public service list can be published to your workshop website, providing clients with a transparent online price list without endless phone consultations.
  • Profitability Analytics: The system correlates performed services against revenue, allowing the owner to see which repair categories drive genuine net margin and which represent low-margin busywork.

Master Craftsman Advice: Audit Your Catalog Quarterly

Avoid cluttering your active registry with one-off custom requests logged years ago. Archive obsolete jobs and compare standardized labor hours against stopwatch realities at the workplace every quarter. If an operation consistently takes 2.5 hours instead of the budgeted 1.5 hours, update the standard promptly to avoid working at a loss.

Troubleshooting & Common Pitfalls ​

What should I do if service cost does not update when editing labor hours?

Verify the pricing mode selected on the service card: if "Fixed Price" is enabled, labor duration and hourly master rates are ignored. Switch the toggle to "Calculated" mode so total pricing recalculates dynamically via the formula engine.

Can I delete a service listed on an older order?

Yes. Once you confirm, the service disappears from the catalog. Existing order line items remain available.

How can I update the hourly rate for all calculated services at once?

Navigate to Space Settings under the "Settings" tab and update the default master hourly rate. All calculated services throughout your catalog and subsequent work orders will immediately adopt the updated rate.
