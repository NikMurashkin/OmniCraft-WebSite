Property & Resources: Unified Tangible Asset Registry ​

The Property & Resources tab consolidates all tangible physical assets owned by the craftsman into a single, unified material registry. Unlike operational cash on bank accounts or exchange-traded securities, this section tracks long-term tangible assets: residential and commercial real estate, storage facilities, workshop studio spaces, commercial utility vehicles, heavy machinery, cutting and sewing systems, precision hand tools, and strategic warehouse stocks of raw materials.

Tangible assets form the physical bedrock of any craft enterprise. A craftsman's true capitalization is determined not merely by liquid funds in checking accounts, but by the combined fair market valuation of their production equipment and personal holdings that safeguard workshop stability.

Tangible Funds Architecture ​

Physical assets in OmniCraft ERP are categorized into four foundational asset classes:

  • Real Estate & Land:
    Workshops, storage facilities, commercial and residential properties, and development plots.
  • Vehicles:
    Commercial transport vans, logistics trailers, and personal vehicles used for business.
  • Equipment & Machinery:
    Industrial sewing machines, CNC lasers, precision cutters, workbenches, and specialized tooling.
  • Materials & Inventory:
    Warehouse stocks of raw materials, metal hardware, packaging supplies, and craft chemicals.

This consolidation bridges the gap between workshop operations and the master balance sheet: the software links production and service facility assets directly to the owner's personal net worth, preventing loss of oversight over high-value machinery and accumulated inventory.

Category Segment Filter ​

Directly beneath the KPI cards, a four-segment filter switch allows instant registry filtering:

  • "All Positions": complete uninterrupted view of all physical assets;
  • "Real Estate & Vehicles": filtered listing of land, buildings, and transport;
  • "Equipment & Fixed Assets": dedicated inventory of machinery and tools;
  • "Materials & Inventory": warehouse stockpiles with itemized valuations.

Separating Liquidity from Tangible Capital

Never confuse working capital in checking accounts with the value of workshop machinery. A leather splitter or CNC laser cannot be instantaneously liquidated to purchase a batch of hides or settle rent without halting workshop production. Maintaining clean segregation between liquid accounts and tangible property prevents the illusion of excess cash.

Three-Way Ownership Share Model ​

The core architectural breakthrough of the Property & Resources module is native support for shared ownership and multi-workspace allocation across personal life and business lines.

A single physical asset often serves the craftsman's family, assists in batch production, and supports on-site service calls. For every item, you configure a precise allocation ratio:

[Personal Use] + [Production Workspace] + [Service Center] = 100.00%

When equipment or workspace premises are acquired jointly with business partners, OmniCraft ERP factors consolidated Net Worth strictly proportional to your personal equity percentage. Depreciation expenses are proportionally distributed across workshops according to assigned utilization quotas.

Hamilton Largest Remainder Balancing ​

The ownership share editor incorporates the Hamilton largest remainder algorithm. When you modify the share percentage for one sector, the system automatically recalculates the remaining values, ensuring the total stays locked at 100.00% (10,000 basis points) without rounding drift.

Workshop Practical Case:

A craftsman purchases a heavy-duty commercial utility van for $45,000:

  1. Personal Use (40%): family travel, personal commutes, and domestic errands;
  2. Production Workspace (40%): hauling wholesale hides and timber from suppliers and delivering wholesale product batches;
  3. Service Center (20%): mobile diagnostic trips, on-site installations, and customer maintenance visits.

The owner's consolidated balance sheet captures the vehicle according to personal ownership equity, while recurring maintenance and depreciation charges are automatically expensed across production and service operations based on these established quotas.

Conservative Liquidation Valuation

For industrial machinery and vehicles, consistently record a conservative liquidation figure—the cash amount you could realistically realize within two to three weeks under urgent liquidity requirements. Inflating valuations breeds false security and skews managerial reporting.

Security Collision Audit & Fund Normalization ​

When a craftsman logs tools inside the production workshop while simultaneously tracking personal property, duplicate entries can easily occur: a CNC laser could mistakenly exist both as a workshop fixed asset and as personal property.

To perform a comprehensive registry review, click "🛡️ Check Duplicates & Collisions". The "Funds & Collisions Audit" dialog inspects data integrity across three areas:

  1. Ownership Share Collisions: scans all active records for allocation totals diverging from 100.00%. If shares exceed or fall below 100% (e.g., 140% due to parallel workspace edits), the card highlights the discrepancy. Clicking "Fix Shares (100%)" automatically normalizes ratios back to equilibrium (10,000 basis points in the underlying database).
  2. Potential Duplicate Equipment: compares personal asset titles against the Workshop / Assets registry, displaying paired cards and detection rationales. Clicking "Merge Duplicates" merges redundant cards into a single master record while preserving historical operation logs and recipe linkages.
  3. Potential Duplicate Raw Materials: locates overlapping entries between personal inventory holdings and operational warehouse stock with safe consolidation.

If no discrepancies are detected, the system displays: "No collisions or duplicates detected. All funds are reconciled."

Regular Audit Cadence

Run the collision audit following major equipment acquisitions, workshop reorganizations, or during annual physical inventory counts. This guarantees that balance sheet equity reflects authentic physical assets without phantom duplicates.

Smart Delete Guard & Retirement Standards ​

Physical assets are deeply integrated with workshop operations: machinery feeds hourly rate calculations in recipe engineering, while raw materials are expensed during workbench production batches.

OmniCraft ERP enforces an intelligent deletion guardrail:

  1. Clean Record Without History: if an asset card was entered erroneously (typo, test record) and has no associated ledger or warehouse entries, the application requests standard soft deletion confirmation.
  2. Record with Transaction History: if an asset was utilized in active operations (acquisition, depreciation, maintenance, production write-offs, cashflow entries), direct deletion is intercepted by "Deletion Blocked: History Detected". The system itemizes detected dependencies and provides safe archival with required disposition reason:
    • "Sold / Realized"—asset was liquidated with incoming proceeds;
    • "Scrapped / Worn Out"—asset was retired due to mechanical or technological obsolescence;
    • "Gifted / Decommissioned"—asset was transferred out of operational cycles.
  3. Preserving Analytical Integrity: clicking "Send to Archive" removes the card from active operational views while leaving historical recipe calculations, retrospective reports, and cashflow ledgers intact. When selling machinery, liquidation proceeds route directly into an active wallet in the "Accounts & Wallets" tab.

Preserving Historical Analytics

Never attempt to erase or force-delete retired equipment cards. Archiving maintains audit trails for tax compliance and historical managerial accounting, preserving retroactive reporting integrity.

Troubleshooting & Common Pitfalls ​

What should I do if the audit detects ownership share collisions (shares do not equal 100%)?

Inside the "Collision Audit" dialog, open the collision section and click "Fix Shares (100%)". The engine automatically normalizes allocations across Personal, Production, and Service domains to exactly 10,000 basis points.

Why does the system lock deletion for a machine or equipment card?

The two-stage smart delete guard blocks hard removal because the machine is referenced in product recipe calculations or open service tickets. Click "Delete / Retire" and designate a retirement reason ("Sold" or "Decommissioned") to archive the asset safely.

How do I merge machinery logged concurrently as personal property and workshop equipment?

Launch the audit via the "Collision Audit" toolbar button. Under the equipment duplicate section, click "Merge Duplicates": the system unifies both records into a single master card with consolidated ownership shares.
