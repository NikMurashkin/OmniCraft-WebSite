Analytics & Currency Basket: Strategic Wealth Control ​

While operational screens — such as the workshop cash flow ledger or the bank accounts registry — answer the immediate tactical question of "How much liquid cash is available right now?", the analytical engine within the "My Capital" workspace resolves a fundamentally different strategic challenge. It provides the creator with a clear, long-term answer: how securely is your consolidated wealth protected against currency devaluations, inflationary shocks, and market crises, and is your family capital steadily growing year-over-year?

The system provides two complementary analytical tools:

  • "Capital Statistics" Quick Widget: opened directly from the accounts and investments toolbar for immediate verification of currency diversification and asset fund balances;
  • "Analytics" Strategic Tab: the fifth element in the top navigation funnel, dedicated to tracking multi-year net worth trajectories and monitoring household burn rate trends.

Display Modes: Side Drawer or Centered Modal Dialog ​

The interface adapts to your workspace layout. In the "Workspace Settings" dialog (gear icon on the toolbar), you can choose your preferred widget format:

  • Side Navigation Drawer (Default): The panel smoothly slides in from the right edge of the screen at a fixed width without obscuring your account lists and investment tables. This is well-suited for widescreen monitors, allowing you to audit currency shares while simultaneously allocating deposits. Close it by clicking the close icon or outside the drawer.
  • Centered Modal Dialog: A focused 520×520 pixel modal window positioned directly in the center of the display. Ideal for deliberate balance audits before closing an accounting period or committing to significant capital purchases.

Safe Handling of Empty States

If the system contains no accounts with non-zero balances and connected workshop databases are empty, the widget displays an informative fallback notice: "Not enough data to calculate statistics". Real-time calculations activate automatically as soon as initial balances are entered.

Currency Basket Allocation: Protecting Capital Against Devaluation ​

Rapid inflation and foreign exchange volatility can erode workshop profit in a matter of weeks. To prevent silent losses, OmniCraft ERP continuously recalculates your Currency Basket.

The algorithm scans every active (non-archived) account in the "Accounts & Wallets" subtab along with open positions in your investment portfolio:

Currency Basket Algorithm
Calculating Currency Share in Liquid Wealth
Currency Share (%)=(Native Balance×Exchange Rate)/Total Liquid Pool×100%

The total liquid pool includes balances across all checking accounts, deposits, cash reserves, and current investment valuations converted into the base accounting currency using active exchange rates.

Multi-Segment Stacked Bar ​

At the top of the section, an interactive multi-segment stacked bar visualizes currency weights. Each segment represents a distinct currency, with width precisely proportional to its share in the liquid pool.

Hovering over any segment displays an informative tooltip showing exact base currency amounts and percentage shares.

Golden Rule of Craft Diversification

A seasoned workshop master structures liquid reserves across three distinct tiers:

  1. Operational Tier (1–2 months of shop expenses): held strictly in the primary currency required by raw material and hardware vendors to ensure seamless inventory replenishment;
  2. Family Safety Cushion (3–6 months of living expenses): maintained in stable, highly liquid currencies and yield-bearing cash deposits;
  3. Growth Capital: distributed between high-efficiency production equipment and a diversified investment portfolio.

Four Asset Classes: Balancing Long-Term Stability ​

While the currency basket analyzes liquid money and securities, the "Asset Classes" panel provides a complete management balance sheet. It categorizes your consolidated net worth into four foundational asset pillars:

  • Liquid Accounts (Teal):
    Checking accounts, high-yield deposits, digital wallets, and cash reserves.
  • Investments (Violet):
    Bonds, equities, index funds, peer loans, and digital assets.
  • Personal Property (Amber):
    Real estate, land, and vehicles weighted strictly by personal ownership share.
  • Workshop Capital (Blue):
    Clean commercial net tangible assets across active production shops and studios.

Net Worth Valuation and Class Weight Formula ​

The program reconciles each asset category against total consolidated capital:

Balance Sheet Allocation
Calculating Asset Class Weights
Fund Share (%)=Class Book Value/Consolidated Net Worth×100%

Consolidated Net Worth = Liquid Accounts + Investments + Personal Property + Active Workshop Assets. Workshop valuation includes cash on hand, raw material inventory calculated via Moving Average Cost (MAC), finished goods via First-In, First-Out (FIFO), and machinery book value net of linear depreciation.

Empty State Protection ​

Financial History Continuity: Architectural Law (Stage M5) ​

One of the most frustrating flaws of conventional accounting software is data loss when restructuring business units. When an artisan sells a woodwork division or discontinues a repair services branch and deletes that workspace, standard tools lose historical records: multi-year profit charts collapse, and financial trajectories become fragmented.

OmniCraft ERP enforces the architectural invariant of financial history continuity (Stage M5).

Financial History Continuity (Stage M5)

OmniCraft ERP automatically records monthly snapshots of your workshops. Even when closing or deleting an old workspace, historical charts preserve every month.

How Historical Snapshot Preservation Operates ​

  • Automated Monthly Snapshots: At the conclusion of each accounting period, the system logs consolidated balance snapshots capturing workshop assets and net profit.
  • Isolation from Workspace Deletion: If a workspace is decommissioned and deleted from disk, all accumulated monthly snapshots are preserved inside the central database of "My Capital".
  • Unbroken Net Worth Trajectory: Multi-year wealth trajectories spanning 3, 5, or 10 years remain continuous. The program accurately accounts for every dollar generated, regardless of corporate or workspace restructuring.

Safe Workspace Archiving

When removing a workspace via workspace management, the system performs a final net asset audit before archiving. All completed calculations remain permanently archived in your family wealth trajectory.

Frequently Asked Questions ​

How frequently does the program refresh statistical metrics? ​

The currency basket and asset class allocations recalculate dynamically each time you open the widget or slide drawer. The system retrieves real-time balances, active exchange rates, and current workshop values directly from local databases.

What happens when currency exchange rates change? ​

When you update exchange rates in the "Exchange Rates" dialog (bidirectional arrow button on the toolbar), all base currency equivalents for foreign accounts and assets update immediately. Relative portfolio shares in the currency basket adjust dynamically.

Are liabilities and debts factored into asset classes? ​

Yes. Outstanding liabilities or negative credit card balances reduce the net liquid cash total. In commercial workshops, accounts payable to suppliers similarly decrease the net asset valuation of that workspace.
