Personal Operations ​

The Personal Operations tab is the primary ledger for significant financial events in the maker's personal contour. It is designed not for small everyday receipts (which are captured automatically by the mathematical delta), but for recording major targeted money movements that shape your overall financial trajectory.

Operations Ledger Philosophy ​

OmniCraft ERP respects your time as an artisan. We never ask you to record every cup of coffee or fuel stop manually.

Only structural transactions belong in this ledger:

  • Business Dividends & Withdrawals: transferring net profits from the workshop's cash flow into your personal bank account.
  • Capital Injections: personal loans or equity infusions deposited into the workshop cash register to purchase equipment or raw materials.
  • External Major Income: rental real estate proceeds, external consulting honorariums, or vehicle sales.
  • Major Capital Expenses: property acquisitions, medical procedures, home renovations, or automobile purchases.
  • Transfers Between Accounts: currency conversions, funding brokerage accounts, or moving money into hardware crypto vaults.

Protecting Delta Integrity

Recording a major windfall (such as selling a personal vehicle) informs the algorithm of the balance jump, ensuring automated workshop expense tracking remains accurate and unskewed.

Logging an Operation ​

To register a transaction, click "Add Operation":

  1. Operation Type:
    • Income: personal wealth increase from external sources.
    • Expense: major targeted personal outflow.
    • Transfer: neutral money movement between owned accounts or currency exchanges.
  2. Amount & Currency: transactional sum and currency denomination.
  3. From / To Account: select the originating and receiving accounts.
  4. Category: select from built-in articles ("Business Dividends", "Investments", "Major Purchases", "Taxes", "Family").
  5. Date & Time: timestamp of the payment.
  6. Notes: descriptive context (e.g., "Advance payment for bespoke workshop consulting").

Filtering and Audit Views ​

Toolbar filters enable quick review:

  • Time Horizon: filter by current month, quarter, year, or custom range.
  • Category & Type: isolate income inflows or major outflows.
  • Search: rapid search by counterparty name or note keywords.
