Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

What should I do if the license key was not delivered to my email?

Immediately after a successful payment, your license token (formatted as `LIC-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`) is displayed directly on the confirmation screen, and a copy is automatically sent to the email address you specified. If you do not see the email within 2–3 minutes, please check your Spam or Promotions folder. If you still cannot locate it, reach out via Telegram or through our contacts page with your payment email and approximate time — we will verify the gateway log and provide your key promptly.

How do I activate the software on a computer without an internet connection?

OmniCraft ERP fully supports isolated workshop environments without continuous internet access. For offline activation, launch the application on your target PC, open Settings ➔ License, and copy the displayed Hardware ID (HWID). Then, using any device with internet access, navigate to the "Manage License" tab on this page, log in with your token, and generate a permanent offline activation key in the "Offline Activation" section.

Can I transfer my license when replacing or upgrading my computer?

Yes. Each commercial license supports up to 5 concurrent devices (e.g., your workshop PC, home laptop, and portable tablet). If you upgrade your computer or reinstall the OS, simply visit the "Manage License" tab on this page, find the outdated device in the list of bound slots, and unbind it to free up a slot for your new workstation.

What happens to my data and database if my monthly subscription expires?

All your commercial records, recipes, warehouse balances, customer history, and financial statements remain permanently intact on your local drive. The software never restricts viewing history, printing documents, or exporting databases. When a paid period ends, only recording new sales beyond the trial threshold is paused until you renew the subscription or upgrade to a lifetime license.
