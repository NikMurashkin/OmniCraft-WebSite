Interface in Detail

Production Accounting.
You don't need to be an accountant

To keep records, you don't need any special education. It is enough to enter basic data: your assets, raw materials purchases, and mark sales. The program takes care of all routine math, protecting you from calculation errors and saving you from confusing spreadsheets.

Assets and Supply

The program calculates the current value of equipment taking into account its depreciation. When purchasing a batch of materials, the system automatically distributes logistics costs to the cost of raw materials.

Cost and Prices

Accurate calculation of product cost based on created recipes (tech cards) taking into account overheads. Automatic calculation of recommended prices for different sales channels.

Transparent Production

A tool for clear planning of the work queue. At the moment of batch completion, the smart workbench automatically deducts reserved materials from the warehouse.

Finance and Analytics

Strict tracking of cash flow (DDS). The system independently compiles the management balance of the workshop and calculates useful business metrics for analysis.

A Single Continuous Flow

One action automatically updates the entire system. No double data entry.

01

Supply

Receipt of assets (with auto-depreciation calculation) and batch purchase of raw materials for the warehouse.

02

Calculation

Creating an item recipe based on your hourly rate, standard hours, and overhead expenses.

03

Production

Completing production automatically deducts raw materials from the warehouse and adds finished goods to the showcase.

04

Sale

Ringing up a sale on the POS terminal deducts the item and makes a double-entry of revenue in the cash flow journal (DDS).

Multi-workspaces and Goals

Manage an unlimited number of independent businesses (e.g., leathercraft workshop and electronic pattern sales) in isolated databases with different currencies and markups. Create spaces to plan and track major financial or life goals.

Global Dashboard "My Capital"

The program automatically aggregates profits from all your businesses, adds personal accounts, and calculates a single metric: Net Worth (Total Capital). You see the actual growth rate of your wealth.

Freedom from the Routine of Personal Expenses

No more entering every receipt for coffee or groceries. Just update the actual balances of your bank cards in the program. The system calculates the delta between earnings and the current balance, automatically showing your daily living expenses.

Frequently Asked Questions

I keep records in Excel. Is it hard to migrate to OmniCraft ERP?

The transition takes very little time. You don't need to import your entire sales history. Just use an "estimated starting point" approach: enter your current equipment in the Workshop, list your actual raw material stock on the Warehouse tab, and specify your historical balance in the cash flow (DDS). The program will pick up the math and begin accurate tracking from that day forward.

What if my computer breaks down?

The program has a powerful system of autonomous backups. In addition to automatic shadow copies, you can export your entire database to a compressed backup archive with 1 click and save it in the cloud or on a USB drive. When buying a new PC, just install the program and import this archive — your entire ecosystem will recover in seconds.

Is there a Mac OS or Linux version?

Currently, we compile the installer only for Windows. However, the system's core is completely cross-platform. Development and release of native versions for Mac OS and Linux are planned in future updates.
