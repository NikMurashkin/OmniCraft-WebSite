Operational Scale in OmniCraft

Software for Makers, Workshop Owners, and Business-Minded Creators

OmniCraft ERP is not just a checkbox accounting tool. It is an end-to-end management ecosystem designed to run your business and consolidate all your finances in one place. The system adapts to your current journey: focus strictly on a single operational domain or deploy comprehensive tracking across multiple projects and capital.

MODE 1 / CORE FOCUS

Single Chosen Domain

Focused management within a single craft. An isolated workspace equipped precisely for your operational workflow:

Artisan Manufacturing

For hands-on makers: leathercrafters, woodworkers, ceramicists, jewelers, chandlers. Clean tracking of raw materials, recipes, and true unit cost.

Service & Repairs

For specialists across any services: repairs, on-site maintenance, restoration, or custom client jobs. Streamlined work orders, labor rate tracking, and direct supply write-offs.

Digital Goods & Products

For creators of courses, masterclasses, 3D models, patterns, cut files, or software. High-margin digital sales tracking with zero inventory, raw materials, or shipping overhead.

Retail Trade & Showroom

For sellers and retailers of finished goods: buying and reselling without in-house manufacturing. Batch inventory, markup management, and a ready web store.

Hobby & Personal Finances

Crafting for pleasure and creative experiments. Effortless tracking of tools and materials with seamless upgrade into commercial sales without data loss.

MODE 2 / MULTI

Project Ecosystem & Wealth

When a single venture becomes too confined or you think with an entrepreneurial mindset. Separate operations and unite capital:

Vertical Expansion & Niches

For makers combining multiple tracks: handcraft production, repair services, and digital patterns within a single synchronized ecosystem.

Parallel Businesses

For entrepreneurs running multiple ventures. Separate accounting for each project's cash, inventory, and clients, unified under single management.

Investment Portfolio & Passive Income

Create and track a multi-asset portfolio: securities, funds, brokerage accounts, loans, business stakes, and yield assets with return and dividend monitoring.

Personal Accounts, Real Estate & Reserves

Family savings, reserves, property, and vehicles. Clear separation of personal wallet and business cash, as well as personal property from workshop equipment and tools.

Consolidated "My Capital" Hub

A complete picture of your wealth: unifies workshops, equipment, inventory, investments, and personal accounts into a single Net Worth balance. See the true value of everything you own with flawless separation of personal and business.

Zero-Bureaucracy Accounting

Mixed Finances

You don't need a separate business account or artificial transfers to yourself "for living" to bring crystal-clear order. Pay for materials, parts, or tools from your personal card and spend revenue on everyday needs—the system automatically and accurately isolates personal and workshop funds.

01. Capital Investment

Paying for Materials, Parts & Tools from Your Card

"Bought supplies, components, fasteners, or a new tool using your everyday personal card..."

No self-loan contracts or complex journal entries. A negative Cash Flow balance in the system is not an error, but an honest counter of your personal investments in the business. With each product sale or completed job, the balance approaches zero, tracking the exact day your investments achieve full payback.

Automatic payback tracking in Cash Flow
02. Maker's Income

Everyday Living Expenses Without Receipt Chores

"Customer payment for an order or completed job hits your card and is immediately spent on family, groceries, or fuel..."

The software strictly isolates your net profit from your card balance. Profit is recorded per completed sale or work order (revenue minus materials cost, overhead, and wear). You always know what your business earned without the burden of logging grocery receipts.

Net profit strictly isolated from living costs
03. True Balance

Enterprise Net Worth at Any Card Balance

"All free cash is reinvested into materials, components, and active orders, leaving zero cash on the card..."

Management balance ("Capital") instantly aggregates all tangible assets: equipment and tools adjusted for wear, materials and components at moving average cost (MAC), finished goods, and cash. You always know the true net worth of your craft even when funds are fully tied up.

Equipment + Inventory + Orders + Cash

Contacts & Support
01I keep records in spreadsheets. Is it hard to switch to OmniCraft ERP?

You don't need to transfer years of old sales history. Simply set an initial starting point: enter actual raw material balances into the Warehouse, add equipment and tools to the Workshop (marking them as "Non-cash receipt / Owner contribution" so funds aren't deducted from cash again), and record your current cash balance. Read more in the Quick Start guide.

02Do I need a constant internet connection to run the program?

No, the program operates completely offline. The database is stored locally on your computer, so you can track stock, process sales, and manage production without network access. Internet is only required to check for updates and activate your license key.

03Where is my data stored and do developers have access to the database?

All data is stored exclusively on your computer in a local database. The program does not collect or upload revenue, customer base, recipes, or inventory to external servers. Only the database owner has access to commercial information.

04What happens if my computer breaks?

Built-in shadow copies protect data from sudden power loss. In case of physical disk failure, it is recommended to regularly save a backup copy of your database to secure personal cloud storage or an external drive. On a new computer, simply restore the database from the backup file. The license can be freely rebound to the new PC via the website (up to 5 computers supported).

05Can I work with the database from different computers?

Currently, the database is stored strictly locally on your computer. If needed, you can manually transfer the database file between devices (e.g., via private cloud storage or an external drive). Built-in multi-device synchronization is planned for future updates.

06What happens when the 20-sale limit runs out in the free version?

The program pauses processing new sales. All other features remain fully accessible: you can add materials, manage stock, edit recipes, and view analytics. Your data is never deleted, and the sales limit is removed automatically upon entering a license key.

07Do I need to pay for software updates?

No. All subsequent program updates and bug fixes are provided completely free of charge.

08Is there a mobile version for smartphones?

It is planned for the future. Currently, the program is built for Windows PCs, but mobile app development is on our roadmap.
