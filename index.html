Fundamental problems we solved

Whether you run a single craft workshop or manage multiple businesses, the system eliminates major management mistakes and operational friction.

Automation and eliminating routine

Freedom from manual calculations and routine spreadsheets

End-to-end operations and error protection

The accounting process is organized as a strict end-to-end pipeline. A single click to complete a stage automatically deducts the used raw materials, credits the finished product to the showcase, and locks in its actual cost price, reliably protecting data from accidental errors.

Smart cost allocation without a calculator

You don't need to be an accountant. The program automatically calculates the Moving Average Cost (MAC) of materials with each new purchase, allocates logistics costs, and helps evaluate equipment wear (from hand tools to CNC machines) for precise overhead planning.

No more surprises at the workbench

You will see a material deficit warning even before you start an order or take an advance payment. The dynamic queue automatically reserves the necessary hardware, wood, or specific chemicals in the warehouse based on task priority.

Honest finances and clear analytics

Efficient and effortless control

Eliminating the "Illusion of Profit"

The difference between the sale price and the cost of the main material is not net profit. The calculator will help combine all costs: raw materials, overheads (equipment depreciation, rent, consumables), defect rate, and the price of your working hours, revealing the real, honest net profit of each item.

Net profit per hour of work

The Time ROI ranking visually shows that mass-producing candles yields $30/hr, while creating a complex custom piece yields only $5/hr. This is a precise mathematical signal to revise prices or optimize your product range.

Focused business analytics

You have access to deep, detailed analytics. The program provides general analytics and useful data on individual workflow tabs. Each metric holds logical value, covering both core and important supplementary parameters of your business, excluding unsystematic data to avoid turning analytics into chaos.

Protecting old batches from depreciation

Even if suppliers double raw material prices tomorrow, the program will write off the sold old item from the shelf at its original, historically accurate cost (FIFO method). Your profit in financial reports will always be calculated with pinpoint precision.

And much more...

OmniCraft ERP capabilities go beyond simple production tracking. The system allows inventory audits without distorting Cash Flow (DDS) reports, distributing logistics costs (choosing between client payment or workshop expense), flexible pricing for different sales channels, and maintaining a structured Cash Flow register for tax calculation.

Frequently Asked Questions

I keep records in Excel. Is it hard to switch to OmniCraft ERP?

The transition takes minimal time. You don't need to transfer your entire history of old sales. Simply use the "estimated starting point" approach: enter your current equipment into the Workshop, making sure to mark it as a "Gratuitous receipt" (so the program doesn't deduct funds from Cash Flow again, as these expenses were already accounted for in the past), add the actual raw material balances to the Warehouse, and perform a special starting corrective operation to lock in the historical Cash Flow balance and debts. Read more about migrating in the Quick Start section.

What happens if my computer breaks?

Built-in shadow copies (GFS) protect data from sudden power outages, but in the event of a physical drive failure, they will perish along with it. Therefore, we strongly recommend regularly exporting the database manually to a flash drive or the cloud. When moving to a new PC, install the program and import the saved file. The license will need to be restored on a special license management page on the website: since it binds to the hardware, you will need to reset the old binding by entering your license token. The license key supports simultaneous use on up to 5 devices, and you can independently remove old devices from the list and bind new ones.

Is there a version for Mac OS, Linux, or Android?

Currently, official installation packages are built only for Windows OS. However, the system core is designed to be fully cross-platform. The development and release of native versions for Mac OS, Linux, as well as a mobile app for Android, are planned in future updates.

Can I work with the database from different computers?

OmniCraft ERP is designed as a local standalone system for maximum protection of commercial secrets; there is currently no direct online synchronization. In the future, we plan to implement full-fledged cloud data synchronization for seamless collaborative work across different devices.

What happens when the 20-sale limit runs out in the free version?

The program will block sales processing. Meanwhile, all other features remain fully accessible: you can freely add new materials, enter tools and machines into the Workshop, create or edit recipes, populate directories, and analyze past periods. Upon activating a purchased license key, all restrictions will be lifted instantly, and your accumulated history will remain intact.

Can I track multiple different projects in one program?

In the basic "Standard" version, all accounting is conducted within a single production setup. If you have multiple directions (e.g., leather production and a separate hardware store), you will need to upgrade to the PRO mode. It allows creating isolated "Multi-workspaces" with separate warehouses and Cash Flow, aggregating their total profit in the global "My Capital" workspace.

Do I need to pay for software updates when buying a lifetime license?

No, when purchasing a perpetual lifetime license, all subsequent system updates, interface improvements, and bug fixes within your tier are provided absolutely free. The built-in auto-updater will prompt you to install the new version and migrate your database automatically. Only the upgrade to the PRO version and specialized add-on modules are paid separately.
