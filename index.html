Operational Scale in OmniCraft

Software for Makers, Workshop Owners, and Business-Minded Creators

OmniCraft ERP is not just a checkbox accounting tool. It is an end-to-end management ecosystem designed to run your business and consolidate all your finances in one place. The system adapts to your current journey: focus strictly on a single operational domain or deploy comprehensive tracking across multiple projects and capital.

Mixed Finances

You don't need a separate business account or artificial transfers to yourself "for living" to bring crystal-clear order. Pay for materials, parts, or tools from your personal card and spend revenue on everyday needs—the system automatically and accurately isolates personal and workshop funds.

Paying for Materials, Parts & Tools from Your Card

"Bought supplies, components, fasteners, or a new tool using your everyday personal card..."

No self-loan contracts or complex journal entries. A negative Cash Flow balance in the system is not an error, but an honest counter of your personal investments in the business. With each product sale or completed job, the balance approaches zero, tracking the exact day your investments achieve full payback.

Automatic payback tracking in Cash Flow

Everyday Living Expenses Without Receipt Chores

"Customer payment for an order or completed job hits your card and is immediately spent on family, groceries, or fuel..."

The software strictly isolates your net profit from your card balance. Profit is recorded per completed sale or work order (revenue minus materials cost, overhead, and wear). You always know what your business earned without the burden of logging grocery receipts.

Net profit strictly isolated from living costs

Management balance ("Capital") instantly aggregates all tangible assets: equipment and tools adjusted for wear, materials and components at moving average cost (MAC), finished goods, and cash. You always know the true net worth of your craft even when funds are fully tied up.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About Licenses

Clarity in details Clear answers to core questions: from moving legacy spreadsheets to running across 5 devices and lifetime updates.

I keep records in Excel. Is it hard to switch to OmniCraft ERP?

The transition takes minimal time. You don't need to transfer your entire history of old sales. Simply use the "estimated starting point" approach: enter your current equipment into the Workshop, making sure to mark it as a "Non-cash receipt / Owner contribution" (so the program doesn't deduct funds from Cash Flow again, as these expenses were already accounted for in the past), add the actual raw material balances to the Warehouse, and perform a special starting corrective operation to lock in the historical Cash Flow balance and debts. Read more about migrating in the Quick Start section.

What happens if my computer breaks?

Built-in shadow copies (GFS) protect data from sudden power outages, but in the event of a physical drive failure, they will perish along with it. Therefore, we strongly recommend regularly exporting the database manually to a flash drive or the cloud. When moving to a new PC, install the program and import the saved file. The license will need to be restored on a special license management page on the website: since it binds to the hardware, you will need to reset the old binding by entering your license token. The license key supports simultaneous use on up to 5 devices, and you can independently remove old devices from the list and bind new ones.

Is there a version for Mac OS, Linux, or Android?

Currently, official installation packages are built only for Windows OS. However, the system core is designed to be fully cross-platform. The development and release of native versions for Mac OS, Linux, as well as a mobile app for Android, are planned in future updates.

Can I work with the database from different computers?

OmniCraft ERP is designed as a local standalone system for maximum protection of commercial secrets; there is currently no direct online synchronization. In the future, we plan to implement full-fledged cloud data synchronization for seamless collaborative work across different devices.

What happens when the 20-sale limit runs out in the free version?

The program will block sales processing. Meanwhile, all other features remain fully accessible: you can freely add new materials, enter tools and machines into the Workshop, create or edit recipes, populate directories, and analyze past periods. Upon activating a purchased license key, all restrictions will be lifted instantly, and your accumulated history will remain intact.

Can I track multiple different projects in one program?

In the basic "Standard" version, all accounting is conducted within a single production setup. If you have multiple directions (e.g., leather production and a separate hardware store), you will need to upgrade to the PRO mode. It allows creating isolated "Multi-workspaces" with separate warehouses and Cash Flow, aggregating their total profit in the global "My Capital" workspace.

Do I need to pay for software updates when buying a lifetime license?

No, when purchasing a perpetual license, all subsequent system updates, interface improvements, and bug fixes within your tier are provided absolutely free. The built-in auto-updater will prompt you to install the new version and migrate your database automatically. Only the upgrade to the PRO version and specialized add-on modules are paid separately.
