Solving Scaling Issues

How OmniCraft ERP solves fundamental accounting mistakes and daily operational friction.

🎓

No Accounting Skills Needed

Simply enter physical stock levels on the warehouse shelves and mark batch completion at your workbench. The software will distribute shipping costs, recalculate MAC asset valuations, and balance your books automatically.

📉

Eliminating the "Illusion of Profit"

Selling a bag for $100 with $30 material cost is not $70 profit. The calculator automatically bundles consumables, equipment depreciation, and your actual labor hours to reveal your true bottom-line margins.

📦

No Surprise Shortages

Orders are soft-reserved based on a cascading queue system. Material shortage warnings light up before you ever pick up a knife, ensuring you do not commit to custom orders without materials on hand.

⏱️

Evaluating Labor Value (Time ROI)

The Time ROI rating tracks exact net profit earned per hour of work. It instantly flags underpriced time sinks: e.g., showing that a simple cardholder makes $30/hr while a complex backpack makes only $6/hr.

🧮

Immunity to Broken Excel Formulas

Excel sheets are prone to cell deletions and broken references. OmniCraft ERP offers a locked transactional pipeline: one click to complete production handles stock, lists items for sale, and logs historical FIFO costs safely.

🏷️

Precise Old Batches Accounting

FIFO logic freezes manufacturing costs when a batch is finished. If leather prices surge tomorrow, the profit of your pre-made items currently sitting on the shelf remains historically accurate down to the cent.

☁️

Complete Digital Autonomy

Your database lives on your PC (Local-First), ensuring 100% privacy. No online subscriptions to access your records. The database runs on WAL and automatically generates GFS backups to protect against power outages.

Frequently Asked Questions

I use Excel. Is it hard to migrate to OmniCraft ERP?

Migration takes minimal time. You don't have to input years of history. Simply use our "starting point" approach: add your existing assets to the Workshop, log current material quantities, and set your starting Cash Flow balance. The software will take over calculations from that day forward.

What happens if my PC breaks down?

The program includes automated shadow backups (GFS model). Additionally, you can export your entire database and ecosystem as a compressed ZIP file in one click. Restoring on a new PC is as simple as installing the app and importing your ZIP backup.

Why choose a desktop app over a cloud system?

Cloud services can raise subscription fees or shut down, leaving your data inaccessible. OmniCraft ERP is a standalone, local database. Your customer data, records, and calculations belong solely to you, running anywhere without requiring an active internet connection.
